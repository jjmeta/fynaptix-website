04 // BEAR CASE
The Bubble Debate
The revenue gap
OpenAI reportedly spends ~$60B/yr on compute against ~$13B revenue. Sequoia-style analysis says ~$600B of annual AI revenue is needed to justify current capex — and the gap is widening.
Off-balance-sheet obligations
Moody's flags ~$662B of signed-but-not-commenced data-center lease commitments sitting off hyperscaler balance sheets.
Circular financing
NVIDIA investing in its own largest customers, model labs raising from their cloud providers — revenue quality gets murky when the money moves in circles.
Depreciation clock
GPUs depreciate over ~3–5 years. If inference revenue doesn't outgrow fleet depreciation, the correction follows the hardware's replacement schedule. Bulls counter: ~$1.19–1.32 of revenue now arrives per $1 of depreciation.
Concentration everywhere
AI is simultaneously the market's largest index weight, VC's largest allocation (43% to two companies), and this hub's connective thread — a drawdown would hit robotics, nuclear, storage, and quantum sentiment at once.
