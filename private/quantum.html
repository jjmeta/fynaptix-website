04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Years from commercial advantage
Most systems remain R&D-phase; revenue today is mostly government contracts and research access, not commercial workloads. Even IonQ's sharply raised $450-460M FY26 guidance leans heavily on the newly acquired SkyWater foundry business, not organic quantum-services demand.
A sector catalyst doesn't protect against company-specific stumbles
The $300M CHIPS Act news lifted the pure-play trio over 10% intraday on Sep 8, but IonQ gave that entire move back and more the very next day after its own Investor Day drew a mixed analyst reception — ending the week down 7.0% even as Rigetti and D-Wave held roughly flat to higher. Good sector news is not a substitute for company-specific execution risk.
Insiders aren't buying — and the CHIPS deals add a new form of dilution
Insiders at IonQ, Rigetti and D-Wave have collectively sold a net of roughly $863M in stock over the trailing three years, with over $63M in 2026 alone and virtually no insider buying. Now the U.S. government itself holds minority equity stakes in D-Wave, Rigetti and Quantinuum as a condition of this week's $300M in CHIPS Act awards — a new class of shareholder layered on top of an already dilution-heavy funding model.
Dilution as a business model
Pure-plays fund R&D via repeated equity raises; share counts grow relentlessly. D-Wave alone issued 7.1M new shares to the U.S. government this week under its CHIPS award. Quantinuum's post-IPO financials still show a massive gap between $28-32M FY26 guidance and its ~$12.9B market cap, and newly listed Pasqal has now fallen to $7.92 — nearly 60% below its debut-day close in barely two weeks — a reminder that new entrants are still being priced on story, not statements.
Winner may not be listed
IBM/Google/Microsoft or private PsiQuantum could reach fault tolerance first, stranding pure-play premiums. PsiQuantum's own separately finalized $100M CHIPS award (Sep 8) for photonic manufacturing — on top of its Brookhaven National Lab partnership — is a fresh reminder that well-capitalized private players can out-execute rather than race the pure-plays on public markets.
