04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Years from commercial advantage
Most systems remain R&D-phase; revenue today is mostly government contracts and research access, not commercial workloads.
Valuations price in perfection
P/S of 109–836× means decades of flawless execution are already in the price; drawdowns of 50%+ are routine — IonQ/RGTI/QBTS fell 6-8% in a single session on Jul 13, 2026 amid broad risk-off selling.
Insiders aren't buying
Insiders at IonQ, Rigetti and D-Wave have collectively sold ~$988M in stock over five years versus under $4M in insider purchases combined (as of Jul 22, 2026) — a persistent gap between executive selling and executive conviction.
Dilution as a business model
Pure-plays fund R&D via repeated equity raises; share counts grow relentlessly. CHIPS Act awards to Rigetti, D-Wave and others are structured partly as government equity stakes — another layer of dilution on top.
Winner may not be listed
IBM/Google/Microsoft or private Quantinuum/PsiQuantum could reach fault tolerance first, stranding pure-play premiums.
