01 // THE THESIS
What Eclipse Actually Believes
FOUNDING BELIEF
Atoms must become as programmable as bits
Eclipse was founded by Greg Reichow, Tesla's former VP of Production, on the thesis that manufacturing and the physical economy had been overlooked by software investors for 30 years. The same transformation software brought to information industries will come to physical industries — just 15 years later.
INSIDER KNOWLEDGE
The founding team has been inside factories
Greg Reichow built Tesla's Model S production line. That inside view — understanding why factories are hard, why supply chain is complex, why robotics breaks in real environments — gives Eclipse the ability to evaluate industrial companies that most VCs cannot.
MANUFACTURING RENAISSANCE
Reshoring is a 20-year secular trend
The combination of US-China tensions, COVID supply chain disruption, CHIPS Act subsidies and IRA manufacturing credits has made domestic manufacturing a policy priority. Eclipse's investments are positioned to benefit from the decade of reshoring investment now underway.
ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING
Printing atoms will be as transformative as printing information
Eclipse backed Desktop Metal on the thesis that industrial 3D printing — particularly in metals — would transform how parts are designed and manufactured. Additive enables geometries and supply chain models that weren't previously possible.
SUPPLY CHAIN AS SOFTWARE
The trillion-dollar supply chain runs on fax machines
Logistics, demand forecasting, procurement — the supply chain that moves physical goods runs on processes designed before the internet. Eclipse backs companies applying AI to make this system as responsive as software supply chains.
CLIMATE AS TAILWIND
Decarbonising industry is a manufacturing problem
Industrial processes account for ~30% of global emissions and are the hardest to decarbonise. Eclipse sees climate tech and industrial tech as the same bet: the technologies that make manufacturing programmable will also make it clean.

05 // HOW TO READ ECLIPSE
Strengths, Blind Spots & the Honest Assessment
Manufacturing expertise is irreplaceable
Greg Reichow built Tesla's production capacity. Lior Susan ran Tesla's energy business. These aren't investors who took factory tours — they've managed production lines and debugged supply chain failures at scale.
Samsara is a landmark outcome
Samsara's (NYSE: IOT) market cap of $20B+ validates the connected operations thesis. Fleet management, industrial IoT and operational AI for physical businesses is now a proven large commercial market.
Desktop Metal is a cautionary case
Desktop Metal went public via SPAC in 2020 at $2.5B valuation and has seen a dramatic decline since. The additive manufacturing thesis is directionally correct; commercial adoption has been slower than projected.
AUM limits influence at growth stage
At ~$1B AUM, Eclipse can lead early-stage rounds but often can't lead larger growth rounds. In capital-intensive industries, that limitation means they sometimes lose board influence when companies face their hardest scaling challenges.
How to use Eclipse for your hub
Eclipse's connected operations and manufacturing thesis directly underpins the critical minerals and energy storage supply chains your hub tracks.
