04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
A mandatory hearing still isn't a permit
The NRC's Aug 13 Clinch River hearing was only the final procedural step before commissioners vote — the public comment window stays open through Aug 27, and no construction permit has actually issued. Zero advanced SMRs deliver commercial power anywhere in the world; first-of-a-kind builds historically run over budget and schedule.
The flagship SMR name is drawing more Sell calls, not fewer
Citi initiated NuScale coverage at Sell with a $6.50 price target and Texas Capital cut to $12 after Q2 revenue collapsed to $75K from $8M a year ago — even as CEO John Hopkins calls the TVA/ENTRA1 6-8GW talks "extremely encouraging." A still-non-binding mega-deal is being used to offset genuinely bearish sell-side math, and a fresh $750M ATM keeps diluting the float underneath it.
A record backlog didn't save GE Vernova from an earnings miss
GEV's Q2 orders jumped 88% YoY to a record $176B backlog, yet adjusted EPS of $2.47 missed the $3.04 estimate and shares fell 3.5% on Aug 20. Backlog size is not the same as near-term earnings conversion — a caution for any nuclear-adjacent industrial story being valued on order momentum alone.
Even "de-risked" balance sheets aren't immune to cost overruns
Centrus's Q1 net income fell 63.2% YoY on rising Piketon expansion costs, UBS cut its price target to $170 from $195, and shares have fallen from a $464 high to the $176-187 range despite a growing $3.0B HALEU backlog. Meanwhile Oklo's FY26 cash-use guide keeps climbing ($120-150M, up from $80-100M) even after a real first-criticality milestone — profitable-sounding contracts and technical wins don't automatically translate into controlled costs.
