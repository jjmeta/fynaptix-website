04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Nothing is operating yet
Oklo and NuScale are valued on promise; neither has an operating power plant. First-of-a-kind builds historically run over budget and schedule (see Vogtle). The TVA/ENTRA1 6 GW NuScale deal still has no public construction timeline.
Regulatory timelines
NRC's July 2026 licensing-modernization proposal is a multi-year process (45-day comment period just opened); reform doesn't guarantee faster real-world builds, and slips still reprice stocks hard.
Competition for the same demand
Gas turbines, renewables + storage are faster to deploy for data centers; SMRs must win on 2030s economics that are unproven.
Execution skepticism is already showing up in prices
OKLO, SMR, and X-Energy each fell ~8-9% on Jul 16, 2026 amid AI-trade profit-taking; Oklo is down ~41% YTD, Truist initiated a Hold, and Goldman cut its NuScale price target from $9 to $6 — investors are demanding proof of execution, not just narrative.
