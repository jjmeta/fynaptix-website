OpenAI remains reportedly in talks to buy power from Helion (potentially up to 5GW by 2030) alongside the existing Microsoft PPA (50MW by 2029 via the Orion plant, under construction); no new dated news on either commercial deal this week.
Holtec Nuclear
IPO PULLED · CITES MARKET CONDITIONS
Holtec suspended its planned ~$900M Nasdaq listing (ticker HNUC) on Sep 16, per Bloomberg, after targeting a $10.2B valuation just over a week earlier; Barron's called it a sign that "the nuclear euphoria of the past two years has worn off." The S-1 remains on file but no new pricing date has been set. Underlying business is unchanged: $564M trailing revenue anchored by the Palisades restart and the SMR-300 microreactor program.
04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Holtec just pulled a $900M IPO from a company with real revenue
Unlike the pre-revenue SMR developers on this page, Holtec had $564M of trailing revenue and an operating asset (Palisades) — and it still suspended its Nasdaq listing citing "market conditions" days after targeting a $10.2B valuation. If a company with an actual operating base can't get comfortable pricing conditions right now, that's a read on sector-wide investor appetite, not just one company's story.
Even TerraPower's flagship project just lost its lead contractor
Bechtel and TerraPower split on how to proceed with the next construction phase at Kemmerer, the most advanced private SMR project in the country. Both sides are framing it as an orderly transition with no schedule impact, but an EPC contractor walking away from the next phase of the best-funded Natrium plant is a reminder that construction execution risk doesn't disappear just because a company is well capitalized.
A six-day, 13% slide in the largest nuclear fleet operator, with no bad news attached
Constellation beat Q2 estimates and raised full-year guidance, yet shares fell for six consecutive sessions into Sep 18. When a name with real earnings, a raised guide, and an upgraded price target still can't hold a bid, it argues the nuclear-as-AI-power trade remains a sentiment-driven, valuation-sensitive one rather than an all-weather story.
"Closing in on a deal" has described NuScale's TVA/ENTRA1 agreement for over a year
Shares fell to fresh multi-week lows in the $8-9 range even as coverage continued to frame a binding 6GW PPA as imminent. The gap between narrative and a signed, binding contract is the central risk across this entire sector — a non-binding MOU is not revenue, no matter how many times it gets described as close.
