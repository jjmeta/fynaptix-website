04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
A mandatory hearing still isn't a permit
Clinch River's Aug 13 NRC hearing — the furthest-along utility-scale SMR application in the country — is only the final procedural step before commissioners vote; no construction permit has actually issued yet. Zero advanced SMRs deliver commercial power anywhere. First-of-a-kind builds historically run over budget and schedule (see Vogtle).
A founding backer's exit is now complete, and dilution is filling the gap
Fluor's monetization of its NuScale stake finished in June 2026, realizing roughly $2.43B versus $570M invested — an early insider fully cashed out, not just "signaled" an exit. NuScale is now raising up to $750M more via a new ATM program (Aug 11), implying up to ~82M additional shares at an illustrative $9.18 — real dilution funding a still-non-binding 6GW pathway.
Regulatory clearance still isn't construction, and construction still isn't power
TerraPower's Kemmerer permit was a genuine milestone — the first US approval of a commercial non-light-water reactor in 40+ years — yet the plant still won't generate power until roughly 2031. GEV/Blue Energy's 2.5GW Texas project won't reach a final investment decision until 2027, with SMR capacity not arriving until 2032. Multi-year gaps between permit and first power remain the norm across every design in this sector.
Burn rates are still outrunning the hype
Oklo's own FY26 guide points to $120-150M of cash use even after Q2's cost-clarity concerns drove Truist and Canaccord to cut price targets; X-energy's Q2 opex rose 156% YoY against 154% revenue+grants growth, with EPS missing estimates. Uranium spot ($86.48/lb) remains well off >$100 highs seen earlier this year. Pre-revenue names are still deeply loss-making while trading on valuations that assume commercial success that hasn't happened yet.

AUG 13, 2026
Clinch River mandatory NRC hearing convenes in Oak Ridge
NRC's mandatory uncontested hearing on TVA's Clinch River BWRX-300 construction permit was held as scheduled in Oak Ridge, TN, following NRC staff's June safety-evaluation recommendation. Commissioners' formal permit decision — which would be a first for a commercial SMR — is still pending.
AUG 13, 2026
GE Vernova/Blue Energy advance 2.5GW Texas gas-plus-nuclear project
Agreement moves the Victoria, TX project (two 7HA.02 gas turbines plus up to five BWRX-300 SMRs) into engineering, licensing and safety-analysis. ~1GW gas-fired power targeted for a data center in 2030, with 1.5GW of SMR capacity to follow from 2032; final investment decision targeted 2027.
AUG 13, 2026
X-energy posts Q2: revenue+grants up 154%, but EPS misses
Revenue and grant income rose to $54.6M as DOE ARDP engineering activity ramped, but operating expenses rose 156% to $164.6M and EPS of -$0.15 missed the -$0.09 estimate. DOE increased its ARDP cost-share commitment by up to $1B, bringing the total to $2.115B for the Dow project and TX-1 fuel facility.

NuScale filed a new at-the-market program for up to $750M of Class A shares (~82M shares at an illustrative $9.18). The raise follows confirmation that Fluor's monetization of its NuScale stake — flagged as in-progress last edition — fully wrapped in June, realizing ~$2.43B versus $570M invested.
