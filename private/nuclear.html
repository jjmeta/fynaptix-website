04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Nothing is operating yet
Oklo and NuScale are valued on promise; neither has an operating plant. First-of-a-kind builds historically run over budget and schedule (see Vogtle).
Regulatory timelines
NRC licensing is slow even with reform; slips of years are normal and stocks reprice hard on delays.
Competition for the same demand
Gas turbines, renewables + storage are faster to deploy for data centers; SMRs must win on 2030s economics that are unproven.
Hype-cycle valuations
SMR names have already re-rated on AI-power narratives; a single delayed milestone can unwind multi-bagger moves.
