04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
NuScale's revenue didn't just stall — it collapsed
Q2 revenue fell to $75,000 from $8.1M a year earlier, and net loss widened to $50.1M from $37.6M, as the Romania engineering work behind the old figure wrapped up with nothing to replace it. The entire bull case now rests on a still-non-binding TVA/ENTRA1 PPA the CFO expects by year-end — a design certification is not a paying customer.
Oklo is stacking financing risk on top of regulatory risk
A Sept 3 prospectus supplement for a fresh ~$140M at-the-market stock sale, plus continued insider selling, arrived even as shares rebounded off the PJM/FERC interconnection dispute — a reminder that pre-revenue SMR developers fund themselves by diluting shareholders, and that this dilution compounds whatever the underlying regulatory fight costs in delay.
Even the "proven" names aren't immune to valuation compression
BWXT fell another ~8.6% this week to $157.59 (now 33% off its April high) despite unchanged execution — the Janus win, naval contracts, and a closed acquisition. When a name with real backlog and real earnings still de-rates this hard, it's a reminder that nuclear-as-AI-power remains a rate-sensitive, multi-year-payback trade, not a shield against macro repricing.
Centrus keeps re-rating lower despite a genuinely improving deal book
Shares remain down ~28% YTD even after a modest 2% bounce this week, and the market still isn't convinced the Piketon cost overruns behind Q1's 63.2% YoY income decline are fixed — the X-energy supply deal and DOE's $900M task order are real wins that haven't yet translated into a re-rating.
