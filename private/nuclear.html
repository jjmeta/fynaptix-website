04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Nothing is operating yet
Even after Oklo's Jul 23 DOE startup authorization, zero advanced SMRs generate commercial power. Reactor Pilot Program entrants (Antares Nuclear, Valar Atomics, Aalo Atomics) hit criticality around the Jul 4 EO deadline, but those are small demonstration units, not utility-scale, grid-connected SMRs — a real speed proof point, not commercial proof. First-of-a-kind builds historically run over budget and schedule (see Vogtle); the TVA/ENTRA1 6 GW NuScale deal remains non-binding pending a PPA.
Regulatory timelines remain multi-step
NRC's licensing-modernization rule was formally published Jul 16 (comment period open), and Clinch River's construction permit — the furthest-along SMR application — still needs an Aug 13 mandatory hearing before issuance. Reform doesn't guarantee faster real-world builds, and slips still reprice stocks hard.
Competition for the same demand
Gas turbines, renewables + storage are faster to deploy for data centers; SMRs must win on 2030s economics that are unproven, even with hyperscalers now diversifying nuclear bets across Kairos, X-energy, TerraPower and Oklo.
Analysts are cutting targets even as milestones clear
Barclays, Goldman and Truist all trimmed price targets on OKLO and SMR in the past two weeks despite the Groves authorization and continued hyperscaler PPA flow; Oklo fell from a post-approval high near $55 to the low $40s by Jul 26 and URA slid ~3% on Jul 24 — investors are demanding proof of execution, not just regulatory wins.
05 // CATALYSTS
News & Upcoming Events
JUL 23, 2026
Oklo receives DOE startup authorization for Groves reactor
Clears the way for fuel loading, startup testing and first criticality at the Texas isotope test reactor — just over 10 months from groundbreaking.
JUL 22, 2026
Pilot-program reactors reach criticality ahead of EO deadline
Antares Nuclear, Valar Atomics and Aalo Atomics reached criticality on or before the Jul 4 target set by Executive Order 14301 — the first tangible proof point for DOE's Reactor Pilot Program, though at demonstration scale.
JUL 16, 2026
NRC formally publishes reactor-licensing overhaul
"Modernizing Reactor Licensing, Safety Oversight, and Siting Practices" proposed rule published in the Federal Register (ADVANCE Act implementation); public comment period now open.
JUL 7, 2026
Clinch River construction-permit hearing scheduled
NRC set an Aug 13, 2026 mandatory hearing in Oak Ridge, TN on TVA's BWRX-300 (GEV) construction permit — the final procedural step before a first-of-its-kind permit can be issued.
JUL 10, 2026
Holtec Nuclear files for Nasdaq IPO
S-1 filed for ticker HNUC seeking an estimated ~$2.0B raise; proceeds earmarked for the SMR-300 program — latest entrant in the sector's IPO wave alongside X-energy's April listing.
JUL 22-24, 2026
Sector-wide pullback despite milestones
OKLO slid to the low $40s and URA fell ~3% to $39.89 even as Barclays, Goldman and Truist cut price targets across OKLO/SMR — profit-taking and execution skepticism outweighing the week's regulatory wins.
