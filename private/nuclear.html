04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
A federal interconnection fight just hit a Tier 2 SMR name where it actually operates
PJM's Sept 1 removal of Oklo's 750MW Meta-backed Ohio project from its study queue — over a disputed inverter ride-through data gap — is exactly the kind of grid-bureaucracy risk that pre-revenue SMR developers face well before reactor technology risk ever comes into play. Oklo's own FERC filing warns of a 14-month delay and higher costs; shares fell to ~$38.46 before a partial rebound, still down ~43% YTD.
NuScale's dilution math still isn't settled
The $1B ATM stock-sale program completed in June 2026 funds commercialization, but it also means today's Wall Street-implied upside is diluted before it's realized — Sept 4 coverage flags the risk explicitly even as the stock proved resilient through this week's broader SMR selloff. The CFO's year-end PPA guidance on the 6GW TVA/ENTRA1 project remains non-binding, and NuScale still wouldn't own the reactors in that structure.
Centrus keeps re-rating lower despite a genuinely improving deal book
Shares are down ~28% YTD and fell another ~12% this week to ~$170 even after the X-energy LEU/HALEU supply deal and DOE's $900M Piketon task order — Northland's Q4 EPS estimate cut to $0.67 from $0.77 shows the market still isn't convinced the underlying cost overruns are fixed, and the stock trades near 59x forward earnings on that unresolved question.
Rising yields are compressing every long-duration nuclear growth story at once
This week's sector-wide pullback (URA and URNM both down roughly 5-6% off last week's levels) hit CEG, CCJ and GEV even where company-specific news was neutral-to-positive (Crane FERC waiver, Westinghouse IPO filing) — a reminder that the nuclear-as-AI-power trade is still a rate-sensitive, multi-year-payback story, not a name-picker's shield against macro repricing.
