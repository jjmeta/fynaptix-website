04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
A DOE test-reactor win isn't a commercial SMR
Oklo's Aug 25 authorization to load fuel and operate the Groves Isotope Test Reactor is a genuine technical milestone, but Groves is a non-power test unit, not a revenue-generating plant — shares are still down ~38% YTD and FY26 cash-use guidance keeps climbing ($120-150M). Zero advanced SMRs deliver commercial power anywhere in the world.
NuScale's own numbers keep getting cut even as the headline GW figure grows
RBC cut its price target to $10 from $14 and Citi to $6.50 from $7.50 after Q2 revenue collapsed to $75K, and Aug 28 coverage points out NuScale wouldn't actually own the reactors in the TVA/ENTRA1 structure driving the stock's narrative — a large non-binding GW number is being used to offset genuinely bearish sell-side math.
Record backlogs and fresh supply deals still aren't showing up in earnings
GE Vernova's Q2 adjusted EPS of $2.47 missed the $3.04 estimate despite an 88% YoY jump in orders to a record $176B backlog, and Cameco's own late-July print missed profit expectations even as spot uranium hit a six-month high. Order books and price momentum keep outrunning current-quarter earnings across the sector.
Cost overruns don't disappear just because a new customer signs up
Centrus's fresh LEU/HALEU supply deal with X-energy is a genuine second offtake win, but it doesn't resolve the Piketon cost overruns that drove Q1 net income down 63.2% YoY and pushed UBS's target to $170 from $195 — a new prepayment provides fresh capital, it doesn't retroactively fix a construction-cost problem.
05 // CATALYSTS
News & Upcoming Events
AUG 25, 2026
DOE authorizes Oklo's Groves Isotope Test Reactor, shares jump 8.59%
The Department of Energy authorized Oklo to load fuel, begin testing and operate the privately financed Groves Isotope Test Reactor under the DOE Reactor Pilot Program — a real regulatory milestone, though Groves is a non-power test unit rather than a commercial power plant. Shares jumped 8.59% on the news even as the stock remains down ~38% YTD.
AUG 26, 2026
BWXT wins US Army's Janus microreactor program at Fort Campbell
BWXT was selected to deploy its 20MW BWXT Advanced Nuclear Reactor (BANR) at Fort Campbell, Kentucky, under the Army's Janus program, with construction targeted for late 2028 and operations in the early 2030s — a new government microreactor customer beyond its existing NNSA and naval-reactor work.
AUG 26, 2026
RBC and Citi cut NuScale targets to $10 and $6.50 as Q2 revenue craters to $75K
RBC lowered its price target to $10 from $14 (Sector Perform) and Citi cut to $6.50 from $7.50 (Sell) after Q2 revenue fell to $75,000 from $8M a year ago. Aug 28 coverage separately notes NuScale wouldn't own the reactors in the year-old TVA/ENTRA1 6GW framework its stock narrative leans on.
AUG 19, 2026
Bernstein reiterates Constellation Outperform, $296 target
Bernstein SocGen reaffirmed its Outperform rating and $296 price target on Constellation Energy, following the Aug 6 guidance raise to $11.50-$12.50 adjusted EPS on the back of ~920MW of new long-term nuclear agreements averaging 18.5 years, including Walmart.
AUG 6, 2026
Centrus signs definitive LEU/HALEU supply deal with X-energy
Centrus and X-energy signed a definitive agreement for LEU and HALEU enrichment services to support X-energy's initial Xe-100 SMRs and TRISO-X fuel deployments, with X-energy prepayments providing Centrus non-dilutive capital for its Piketon expansion — the second HALEU offtake after Oklo.
AUG 25, 2026
Uranium spot hits 6-month high as Kazatomprom supply delays bite
TradeTech's U3O8 spot price indicator rose $1.75/lb to $89.50, its highest level in six months, as fresh operational delays at Kazatomprom's TQZ sulphuric-acid plant compound supply concerns already in play from the Cigar Lake recovery and 2026 output cuts.
