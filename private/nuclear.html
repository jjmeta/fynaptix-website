04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
A single sell-side note can still move the whole sector 5% in a day
Piper Sandler's Sep 10 split coverage — Overweight on Oklo, Sell on X-Energy — dragged Oklo, NuScale and X-Energy all down ~5% in the same session, even though only one of the three names actually got downgraded. That correlation is a reminder these stocks still trade as a basket on sentiment, not on company-specific fundamentals.
Oklo is now running back-to-back $1B dilution programs
The Sep 11 equity distribution agreement replaces a May program that had already sold ~17.9M shares for ~$1B — meaning Oklo has authorized roughly $2B of potential share issuance in under five months. Piper Sandler's bullish "bankable by design" thesis is about financing structure, but it doesn't erase the fact that funding a build-own-operate model this early means continuous shareholder dilution.
X-Energy's Sell rating is the first credible bear case with a specific number attached
A $9 price target implies ~53% downside from where shares traded before the note, and the stock has already fallen roughly 15% this week to around $15 — well below its $23 IPO price. The thesis (financing structure determines which SMR developers survive) applies just as much to X-Energy's future fundraising needs as it does to its current $1B+ cash pile.
Even the "proven" names aren't immune to valuation compression
BWXT is still down ~20% over the past 90 days despite winning the Army's Janus microreactor contract for Fort Campbell and posting 18% revenue growth with backlog up 40% YoY. When a name with real backlog and real earnings still trades below its highs, it's a reminder that nuclear-as-AI-power remains a rate-sensitive, multi-year-payback trade, not a shield against macro repricing.
