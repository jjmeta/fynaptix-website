02 // CROSS-CUTTING THEMES
The Threads Connecting Everything
THE AI POWER TRADE
Compute demand → energy infrastructure
AI data centers link nuclear (generation), storage (grid stability), minerals (copper/lithium), and semiconductors. ~$1T US grid capex expected through 2029. One macro bet, four expressions.
PHYSICAL AI
Intelligence gets a body
Robotics, defense autonomy, and space systems share the same stack: NVIDIA compute, foundation models (Skild, Physical Intelligence), actuators and sensors. Component makers sell into all three.
SOVEREIGNTY
Onshoring as investment thesis
Rare earths, defense production, quantum policy, CHIPS funding — governments are now price-setters and demand floors across half these sectors. Policy risk cuts both ways.
HYPE VS FUNDAMENTALS
The valuation divergence
Quantum, humanoids, and SMRs trade on narratives; storage, warehouse automation, and defense backlogs trade on booked revenue. The gap between the two groups is this cycle's defining risk.
IPO WINDOW
Private giants going public
SpaceX (~$2T, June), Agility Robotics SPAC, Unitree/Agibot in China, European defense listings, Generate/Eikon in biotech. Watch lockups and whether debuts hold — it's a sentiment barometer for the whole complex.
