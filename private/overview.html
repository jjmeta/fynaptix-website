03 // CROSS-CUTTING THEMES
The Threads Connecting Everything
CAPEX GETS SELECTIVE
The market stops punishing AI spending across the board
Microsoft (+8%) and Amazon (+10%) were rewarded this week for the same kind of capex hikes that sank Alphabet >7% two weeks earlier; Meta fell ~9–10% anyway despite a revenue beat. The dividing line increasingly looks like visible growth (Azure, AWS) versus capex without one, not capex itself.
PHYSICAL AI
Autonomy keeps commanding private-market premiums
Anduril remains in talks at ~$100B — over 3x last year's mark — while robotics and space names built on the same NVIDIA-compute-plus-foundation-model stack keep attracting capital, even as public pure-plays (AVAV, KTOS) sit 40%+ below their highs.
EXECUTION OVER CATALYSTS
Milestones alone no longer move stocks
Oklo cleared genuine DOE authorization and still slid to a 52-week low pre-earnings; quantum pure-plays fell 30–39% in a month even as D-Wave landed a new AT&T deal. The market wants fuel loading, first criticality and earnings beats — not just headlines.
IPO WINDOW, WOBBLY
Public debuts are testing investor patience
SpaceX traded below its IPO price before its first earnings report as a public company; Generate Biomedicines and Eikon Therapeutics both still trade below their February offers. OpenAI's confidential S-1 sits unmoved as Altman holds a $1T valuation line investors haven't yet agreed to pay.
SOVEREIGNTY
Government money remains the demand floor
USA Rare Earth's Aug 28 shareholder vote, the $5.6B+ federal minerals funding blitz, and the Pentagon's FY27 autonomy budget all point the same direction — Washington is now a price-setter, not just a customer, across nuclear, minerals and defense.
