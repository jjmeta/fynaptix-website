03 // CROSS-CUTTING THEMES
The Threads Connecting Everything
MACRO SHOCK OVERRIDES EVERYTHING
Iran escalation resets the backdrop
Reignited conflict and a Strait of Hormuz-driven oil spike pushed equities into a fourth straight losing session, sent the VIX from ~14.5 to 18.05 (+9.67%) and turned today's August CPI print and the Sept 15-16 FOMC meeting into the biggest swing factor for every rate-sensitive name on this hub — quantum, humanoids and pre-revenue SMRs first and hardest.
UPGRADES CONVERT BACKLOGS
This week's raises share one thread
Alphabet (AI), MP Materials and Symbotic (Robotics), Red Cat and X-energy (Defense/Nuclear), Redwire, AST SpaceMobile (Space), Energy Vault (Storage), 908 Devices (Biotech) and NVIDIA plus IonQ (Quantum) were all raised this week — every one on a converting backlog, a signed offtake, or a partner-validated data point rather than a story alone.
DOWNGRADES SHARE A DILUTION THREAD
Financing and execution risk, not scandal
Unitree (Robotics), AeroVironment (Defense), Oklo (Nuclear), Albemarle (Minerals), Rocket Lab (Space) and QBTS/PSQL (Quantum) were all cut this week — most tied to a fresh dilutive raise, a goodwill impairment, a delayed milestone or a post-debut price break rather than any single scandal.
GOVERNMENT AS PRICE-SETTER, AGAIN
Washington and Beijing keep setting the terms
The FCC's new ban on foreign-made humanoid/quadruped imports, Section 232 drone tariffs now in effect, the Army's $192M TITAN production award to Palantir and Anduril, and China's quiet pause on some US rare-earth shipments are all federal or state decisions doing more to move prices this week than any single earnings report.
IPO CLASS OF 2026 KEEPS DIVERGING
The same bucket holds the year's best and worst outcomes
Anthropic's IPO target has reportedly doubled again toward ~$2T for an October listing on a ~$65B revenue run-rate, while Pasqal has already fallen below its $9.79 SPAC reference price five days after its Aug 28 debut and Unitree remains roughly 50% off its own post-listing peak — the same "class of 2026" containing both outcomes at once.
04 // NEWS OF THE WEEK
Top Cross-Sector Events
SEP 8-11 · MACRO
Iran conflict reignites, oil spikes, equities post a 4-day losing streak
Renewed Strait of Hormuz tanker disruption pushed oil sharply higher and dragged the S&P 500 and Nasdaq to roughly -1.6% week-to-date into today's August CPI report — the last major data point before the Sept 15-16 FOMC meeting, where futures have swung toward meaningful hike risk rather than the cut path priced earlier this year.

Lands the same week Alphabet's Waymo expanded into Denver, San Diego and Tampa (14 cities, 4,000+ vehicles) — concrete monetization evidence even as Oracle's credit was cut to BBB- and Michael Burry's circular-financing short thesis widened to Micron and Nebius.
EARLY SEP · ROBOTICS

Closes off the US market entirely for names like Unitree, compounding a post-IPO slump now near 50% off the Aug 19 peak and reopening the humanoid bubble debate just as Symbotic reaches GAAP profitability on a $721M quarter.
SEP 1-2 · DEFENSE
Army's TITAN program moves to production
A $192M order splits between Palantir ($127M) and Anduril ($65M) the same week AeroVironment lands the US military's first-ever directed-energy production contract ($464.8M) — even as AVAV's own stance gets cut on a goodwill impairment tied to the SCAR/BADGER stop-work.
SEP 3 · QUANTUM
IBM ships Nighthawk r2, its most powerful QPU yet
120 qubits, 218 tunable couplers and 100,000+ circuits/sec — 25x the throughput of the prior Heron system — landing ahead of IBM's own 2026 roadmap milestone and framing manufacturing, not physics, as quantum's remaining bottleneck.
