03 // CROSS-CUTTING THEMES
The Threads Connecting Everything
TARIFF SHOCK
Washington reshapes the drone trade overnight
Trump's Aug 13 proclamation imposing up to 100% tariffs on imported drones lifted AVAV, RCAT, KTOS and ONDS simultaneously across both the Defense and Robotics dashboards — a reminder that a single policy signature can move more names at once than an entire earnings season.
DOWNGRADES CLUSTER AROUND CASH
This week's cuts share a common thread
Oracle (to Avoid), NuScale (to Avoid), Rheinmetall (to Watch), Oklo (trimmed) and Quantum-Si (to Avoid) were all cut this week — not on bad news exactly, but on cash burn, dilution or a founding backer's exit outrunning the growth story. This week's upgrades (Palantir, BAE Systems, L3Harris, Cameco, Energy Fuels) all cite a durable, converting backlog instead.
IPO WAVE, SECOND ACT
The class of 2026 keeps reporting its first real quarters
Quantinuum ($8M revenue, $597M loss), Anthropic (reportedly eyeing an October listing near $2T) and Unitree (trading begins this week after 8,000x oversubscription) show three different stages of the same story — going public doesn't resolve the burn-versus-growth debate, it just puts it on a quarterly clock.
GOVERNMENT AS PRICE-SETTER
Washington keeps setting the terms
Drone tariffs, the Clinch River NRC hearing, a proposed 68% boost to military quantum R&D, and DOE's $900M HALEU award to Centrus all landed the same week — federal decisions are increasingly the swing factor across nuclear, defense, robotics and quantum at once.
QUIET MACRO, LOUD MICRO
A calm tape is masking real single-name dispersion
The VIX sits near 2026 lows (~14.5) and the S&P is hovering close to record highs heading into next week's FOMC minutes and the Aug 27–29 Jackson Hole symposium, but underneath it MP Materials (+23.5%), AeroVironment (+~35% in two weeks) and the $1T shed by chip stocks in late July show the calm index-level read isn't the same as calm underneath.
