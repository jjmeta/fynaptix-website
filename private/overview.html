03 // CROSS-CUTTING THEMES
The Threads Connecting Everything
THE AI POWER TRADE
Compute demand → energy infrastructure
AI data centers link nuclear (generation), storage (grid stability), minerals (copper/lithium), and semiconductors. ~$1T US grid capex expected through 2029. One macro bet, four expressions.
PHYSICAL AI
Intelligence gets a body
Robotics, defense autonomy, and space systems share the same stack: NVIDIA compute, foundation models (Skild, Physical Intelligence), actuators and sensors. Anduril's live-fire CCA missile test this week is the clearest proof yet that autonomy is leaving the demo stage.
SOVEREIGNTY
Onshoring as investment thesis
Rare earths, defense production, quantum policy, CHIPS funding — governments are now price-setters and demand floors across half these sectors. China's entity-list escalation against MP Materials and USA Rare Earth this month shows the policy risk cuts both ways.
HYPE VS FUNDAMENTALS
The valuation divergence
Quantum, humanoids, and SMRs trade on narratives; storage, warehouse automation, and defense backlogs trade on booked revenue. This week the market actively repriced the gap — SMRs and quantum pure-plays both sold off 6-9% while storage and defense backlogs kept setting records.
IPO WINDOW
Private giants going public
SpaceX (~$2T, June) is now down 40%+ from its post-IPO high; Anthropic and OpenAI both sit on confidential S-1s; Quantinuum and X-energy already listed; Holtec Nuclear just filed. Watch lockups and whether debuts hold — it's a sentiment barometer for the whole complex.
