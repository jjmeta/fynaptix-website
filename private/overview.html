03 // CROSS-CUTTING THEMES
The Threads Connecting Everything
CAPEX NOW PUNISHED
The market flips on AI spending
Alphabet beat Q2 estimates but fell >7% — its worst day in over a year — after raising 2026 capex guidance to $195–205B; the Magnificent Seven lost 5.7% in a single week. This week's Microsoft/Meta (Jul 29) and Amazon (Jul 30) reports test whether that punishment dynamic holds across the rest of Big Tech.
PHYSICAL AI
Intelligence gets a body — and a price tag
Robotics, defense autonomy, and space systems share the same stack: NVIDIA compute, foundation models (Skild, Physical Intelligence), actuators and sensors. Anduril in talks at ~$100B — over 3x last year's mark — is the clearest proof yet that private markets are pricing autonomy as a generational bet, even as public pure-plays (AVAV, KTOS) stay repriced 40%+ below highs.
SOVEREIGNTY
Onshoring as investment thesis
Rare earths, defense production, quantum policy, CHIPS funding — governments are now price-setters and demand floors across half these sectors. MP Materials' $900M in new capital (Apple + DoD) and a $2.9B+ federal minerals blitz show the playbook is scaling, even as China's entity-list retaliation shows the policy risk cuts both ways.
HYPE VS FUNDAMENTALS
The valuation divergence
Quantum, humanoids, and SMRs trade on narratives; storage, warehouse automation, and defense backlogs trade on booked revenue. Oklo cleared a real regulatory milestone (DOE startup authorization) and still sold off on profit-taking — proof the market is now demanding execution, not just catalysts, before it pays up.
IPO WINDOW
Private giants going public — and repricing fast
SpaceX is now down ~47% from its post-IPO high; Anthropic and OpenAI both sit on confidential S-1s, with OpenAI reportedly leaning toward a 2027 debut as Altman holds out for $1T; D-Wave just uplisted to Nasdaq. Whether debuts hold remains the best real-time sentiment gauge for the whole complex.
