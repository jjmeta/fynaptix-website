03 // CROSS-CUTTING THEMES
The Threads Connecting Everything
RATE REGIME SHIFT
The Fed's first hike since 2023 resets the backdrop
A unanimous 12-0 vote lifted the federal funds rate 25bp to 3.75%-4% on Sept 16 as oil-driven inflation from the Iran/Hormuz shock forced the Fed's hand; the 10-year spiked as high as ~5.01% on Sept 18, briefly nearing the 2007 record, before easing to ~4.94-4.95% — a genuinely higher-for-longer backdrop that hits quantum, humanoids and pre-revenue SMRs first and hardest.
UPGRADES CONVERT PROOF POINTS
This week's raises share one thread
Oracle (AI), Vishay Precision Group (Robotics), BAE Systems and AeroVironment (Defense), Centrus Energy (Nuclear), Energy Fuels (Minerals), Redwire (Space), Eos Energy (Storage), Illumina and Intellia (Biotech), and Microsoft, IonQ, Rigetti and Quantinuum (Quantum) were all raised this week — every one on a converting contract, a regulatory de-risking step, or a partner-validated technical milestone rather than a story alone.
DOWNGRADES SHARE A MISSED-PROOF-POINT THREAD
Execution and valuation risk, not scandal
Kratos and Red Cat (Defense), L3Harris and Planet Labs (Space), Fluence Energy and Stem (Storage), and NVIDIA's quantum-sleeve position were all cut this week — most tied to a missed leaderboard result, a light guide, sell-side downgrades on margin concerns, or simply a quiet week without a fresh catalyst rather than any single scandal.
THE SPECULATIVE CORNER STARTS REPRICING
Holtec pulls a $900M IPO from a real business
Holtec Nuclear suspended its Nasdaq listing on Sept 16-18 citing "market conditions" days after targeting a $10.2B valuation — notable because, unlike the pre-revenue SMR developers on this hub, Holtec had $564M of trailing revenue and an operating asset (Palisades); a company with an actual operating base stepping back is the clearest tell yet that 2026's frothiest corners are being repriced.
GOVERNMENT AS PRICE-SETTER, AGAIN
Washington and Beijing keep setting the terms
The Fed's rate decision, $400M in newly finalized CHIPS Act quantum awards, the Pentagon's first-ever directed-energy production contract, and Xi Jinping's imminent Washington visit — with rare-earth export licensing explicitly on the agenda — are all federal or state decisions doing more to move prices this week than any single earnings report.
04 // NEWS OF THE WEEK
Top Cross-Sector Events
SEP 16 · MACRO
Fed hikes rates 25bp to 3.75%-4%, first increase since 2023
A unanimous 12-0 vote, with Chair Warsh citing inflation that has run "too high... for too long" following the Iran/Hormuz oil spike; the 10-year briefly touched ~5.01% on the news, nearing the 2007 record, while markets now price at least one more hike before year-end.
SEP 16-18 · NUCLEAR
Holtec Nuclear pulls its ~$900M Nasdaq IPO
Cited "market conditions" days after targeting a $10.2B valuation; unlike pre-revenue SMR peers, Holtec has $564M of trailing revenue and an operating asset (Palisades) — the clearest sign yet that 2026's speculative corners are being repriced.
SEP 18 · MINERALS
MP Materials falls 4.3% as Beijing moves on its Chinese shareholder
Reuters reported state-owned China Rare Earth Group is in talks to acquire Shenghe Resources, MP's 3% shareholder, three days before Xi Jinping's first White House visit in 11 years with rare-earth licensing on the agenda.
SEP 7-9 · QUANTUM
D-Wave, Rigetti, Quantinuum and PsiQuantum finalize $400M in CHIPS Act awards
Each signed a definitive agreement with the Commerce Department, which takes a minority equity stake as a condition of the grant; Quantinuum's Helix architecture separately posted a world-record logical memory error rate the same week.
SEP 9 · DEFENSE
AeroVironment wins the Pentagon's first-ever directed-energy production contract
A $464.8M Army award for LOCUST X3 laser systems lands on top of a Q1 FY27 earnings beat ($480.5M revenue, $1.5B backlog, +37% YoY) — shares have since climbed toward $160, up ~14% from the post-earnings low.
