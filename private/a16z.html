01 // THE THESIS
What Andreessen Horowitz Actually Believes
FOUNDING BELIEF
Software eats every industry
Marc Andreessen's 2011 WSJ essay wasn't just a slogan — it was a fund thesis. a16z bets that every sector has an incumbent whose core processes can be rebuilt in software, and the founders who see it first will own the next generation of that industry.
PLATFORM EDGE
Capital plus a whole back office
Unlike most VCs, a16z employs hundreds of non-investors — recruiters, PR specialists, government-affairs staff, crypto-policy experts — who work exclusively for portfolio companies. The fund competes as a service layer, not just as a cheque.
CRYPTO THESIS
Web3 is the next computing platform
a16z crypto is one of the largest dedicated crypto funds. Their view: blockchains are a new computing architecture that will displace web2 middlemen, and they're willing to wait 10 years for that to manifest — through multiple bear cycles.
AI STANCE
AI is a general-purpose technology
Unlike funds that back AI as a single category, a16z treats AI as a horizontal force: every portfolio company will be AI-native, every sector will be restructured. They invest in AI infrastructure and AI-native applications across all verticals simultaneously.
DEFENSE THESIS
Western democracies have a tech debt
Katherine Boyle's American Dynamism practice invests in companies that support US national security and industrial capacity. The thesis: the US defence establishment has decades of procurement inertia that defence-tech startups can now disrupt with dual-use commercial tech.
TIME HORIZON
Category creation, not category capture
a16z specifically looks for founders creating new categories — not better versions of existing products. They're willing to sit in a position that looks wrong for 3-5 years because the payoff when the category materialises is an order of magnitude larger.
Key Partners: Marc Andreessen · Ben Horowitz · Chris Dixon (Crypto) · Vijay Pande (Bio) · Katherine Boyle (Defense) · Martin Casado (Enterprise)

05 // HOW TO READ ANDREESSEN HOROWITZ
Strengths, Blind Spots & the Honest Assessment
What a16z does better than anyone
The service platform is real and differentiated. Founders cite the talent and go-to-market help as genuinely valuable. The crypto practice is the most credible institutional crypto research operation. Their AI frameworks are sophisticated primary sources.
The portfolio depth is remarkable
No single firm has simultaneously backed GitHub, Coinbase, OpenAI, Stripe and Roblox. Even discounting every forward forecast, the track record of identifying category-winners early across enterprise and consumer is exceptional.
Not every sector call has been right
Consumer social investments have been more mixed than enterprise bets. They've also been aggressive on valuations during zero-rate periods — some 2021 vintage investments are still underwater.
The crypto thesis requires patience
a16z crypto has backed decentralised-everything since 2013. Many protocols remain speculative or illiquid. The macro timing of crypto cycles has repeatedly punished even well-positioned funds.
How to use a16z research
Treat their State of AI and State of Crypto reports as the best free primary research in those categories — rigorously sourced and with no obligation to be bullish on any specific company.
