HUMANOID

ISRG's Q2 beat did not stop the bleeding — shares are down ~34% YTD as multiples compress, and sell-side opinion has split (UBS upgraded to Buy at $500 while HSBC cut to Hold). J&J Ottava and Medtronic Hugo continue to press the competitive case.
DEFENSE
Defense autonomy & drones
AeroVironment's fiscal Q4 revenue more than doubled to $641.6M (Autonomous Systems now 76% of sales) and Kratos beat Q2 estimates while raising FY26 organic growth guidance to 19-23% — backlogs are now visibly converting to revenue. Pentagon FY27 budget still requests ~$75B for unmanned/counter-drone systems.
AG + CONSTRUCTION
Field robotics
Deere's autonomous articulated dump truck continues quarry operations under its 2nd-gen autonomy kit; Caterpillar's five-machine autonomous construction lineup remains in early rollout. Both remain diversified industrials where robotics is a small slice of the return.
JAPAN FA
Japanese factory automation
Sentiment reversed hard: Fanuc shares suffered their worst single-day fall in 40 years on Aug 3 after FY27 guidance (¥218B operating income) missed estimates despite Q1 profit +35% — just weeks after the stock hit limit-up on the same guidance narrative.
COMPONENTS

MP Materials beat Q2 revenue estimates by 13% and expects to begin shipping magnets from its Independence facility in H2 2026 — the transition from miner to magnet maker is now underway.
AI COMPUTE

NVIDIA's Jetson Thor platform now counts 2M+ developers and early adopters including Agility, Amazon Robotics, Boston Dynamics, Caterpillar, Figure, Hexagon, Medtronic and Meta; new T3000/T2000 modules are slated for Q1 2027. 05 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Bubble warnings from serious sources
China's NDRC has warned of a humanoid "bubble"; Morgan Stanley flags a wide gap between hype and scalability. New data sharpens the picture: the median disclosed humanoid round (Aug 2025-Jun 2026) was $145M, with rounds above $50M now 84% of all disclosed capital — and outliers like South Korea's Tomorrow Robotics have jumped 15x in valuation without a single confirmed commercial deployment.
Demos ≠ autonomy
If a humanoid demo isn't explicitly labeled autonomous, assume teleoperation. Dexterity, inference speed, reliability, and cost still confine real use to structured factory/warehouse tasks.
Crowding & shakeout risk
150+ companies chasing humanoids; a reshuffling of startups is widely expected. Robotics pioneer Rodney Brooks argues much of the investment will be wasted.
Guidance can evaporate fast
Fanuc suffered its worst single-day fall in 40 years on Aug 3 despite Q1 profit +35%, purely on FY guidance missing estimates. Serve Robotics cut FY26 revenue guidance 65% (to $9-10M from $26M) after its core Uber partnership saw utilization decline for the first time in 17 quarters. ISRG is down 34% YTD despite beating Q2 estimates. In parts of this sector, sentiment is doing more work than fundamentals — in both directions.
Revenue is elsewhere
Industrial, logistics, and surgical robots generate proven revenue today; humanoids largely don't yet. Component and warehouse names carry less thesis risk than humanoid pure-plays.
China price war
Unitree's $5,900 R1 signals aggressive price compression that could squeeze Western OEM margins before the market matures — and 150+ Chinese humanoid startups are now competing on price.
"Dot-com stage" warnings
KB Securities likened 2026's robotics/space enthusiasm to the final stage of the dot-com bubble; Ray Dalio separately compared the broader AI bubble to 1929 and 2000 valuation extremes. Sector EV/EBITDA multiples ranged from 0.9x (mature industrials) to 78.2x (speculative pure-plays) in Q4 2025 — a valuation split investors should not treat as one trade.
