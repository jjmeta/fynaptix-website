05 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Bubble warnings from serious sources
China's NDRC has warned of a humanoid "bubble"; Morgan Stanley flags a wide gap between hype and scalability. UBS now forecasts only ~30,000 humanoids will ship globally in all of 2026 — a fraction of the volume the funding frenzy implies. The median disclosed humanoid round (Aug 2025-Jun 2026) was $145M, with rounds above $50M now 84% of all disclosed capital.
Private marks don't always hold
Hyundai just paid SoftBank $325M for its last Boston Dynamics stake, implying a $3.3B valuation for one of the industry's most established names — a fraction of Figure's headline $39B mark. Figure's own Forge secondary prices as of Aug 13 imply a softer number than the last primary round too. Private valuations can lag or overshoot reality for a long time before a real transaction corrects them.
Demos ≠ autonomy
If a humanoid demo isn't explicitly labeled autonomous, assume teleoperation. Dexterity, inference speed, reliability, and cost still confine real use to structured factory/warehouse tasks.
Crowding & shakeout risk
150+ companies chasing humanoids; a reshuffling of startups is widely expected. Robotics pioneer Rodney Brooks argues much of the investment will be wasted.
Guidance can evaporate fast
Fanuc suffered its worst single-day fall in 40 years on Aug 3 despite Q1 profit +35%, purely on FY guidance missing estimates — shares have since partly recovered on renewed analyst buy ratings. ISRG is down 32.6% YTD despite beating Q2 estimates. MP Materials rallied 8.6% on management commentary alone, not on the GAAP net loss it just posted. In parts of this sector, sentiment is doing more work than fundamentals — in both directions.
Revenue is elsewhere
Industrial, logistics, and surgical robots generate proven revenue today; humanoids largely don't yet. Component and warehouse names carry less thesis risk than humanoid pure-plays.
China price war
Unitree's $5,900 R1 signals aggressive price compression that could squeeze Western OEM margins before the market matures — and 150+ Chinese humanoid startups, now including BYD, are competing on price and scale.
"Dot-com stage" warnings
KB Securities likened 2026's robotics/space enthusiasm to the final stage of the dot-com bubble; Ray Dalio separately compared the broader AI bubble to 1929 and 2000 valuation extremes. Sector EV/EBITDA multiples ranged from 0.9x (mature industrials) to 78.2x (speculative pure-plays) in Q4 2025 — a valuation split investors should not treat as one trade.
