02 // SUB-SECTOR MAP
Where the Money Is Moving
HUMANOID
General-purpose humanoids
Unitree's post-IPO honeymoon is over: shares slumped from their Aug 19 intraday peak of RMB 1,100 to the RMB 585-605 range by Aug 24-28, a roughly 45% decline that erased close to RMB 200B in market value and drew explicit bubble warnings from analysts, including Bank J. Safra Sarasin's Asia CIO, who called the valuation "probably not justified." Beijing's World Robot Conference and second World Humanoid Robot Games (Aug 19-23) put ~2,000 humanoids on display, with Tiangong Ultra (9.39s) and Honor's Lightning (~9.32-9.47s) both beating Usain Bolt's 100m world record of 9.58s. Tesla's Optimus remains unverified — Musk's July shareholder letter still describes lines "being installed" with output "anticipated later this year."
WAREHOUSE
Warehouse & logistics automation
Symbotic's slide continued: Goldman Sachs cut its price target to $45 from $54 on Aug 25, the same week an SEC filing showed CTO James Kuffner sold roughly 3,952 shares (~$160K), and the stock closed near $40.41 — still digesting last week's dilutive share offering and Barclays' reaffirmed Sell rating despite a beat-and-raise Q3 print. A join-the-club moment (Stanford Robotics Center Industrial Affiliates Program) did little to offset the sentiment.
SURGICAL
Surgical robotics
Intuitive Surgical unveiled a new manufacturing facility in Penang, Malaysia (Aug 20) to expand da Vinci 5 and My Intuitive+ capacity, and picked up reiterated Buy calls from BofA (Aug 11) and Citi ($685 PT, Aug 12). None of it has stopped the slide: shares are still down roughly 33% YTD, on pace for the weakest year since 2008, as J&J's Ottava now carries FDA de novo authorization for a full slate of soft-tissue procedures.
DEFENSE
Defense autonomy & drones
AeroVironment's MacCready Works unit won a contract to co-design and co-manufacture three Mars helicopters for NASA's SkyFall mission, layered on top of a $51M Army order for Switchblade 600 Block 2 munitions and a $100M, 20-acre unified campus planned for Moorpark, CA. Kratos grew Q2 revenue 30.5% YoY with a $2.08B backlog. Yet both AVAV and KTOS shares have traded down in 2026 despite the contract wins — backlog growth and stock performance have decoupled this year.
AG + CONSTRUCTION
Field robotics
Deere's autonomous articulated dump truck continues quarry operations under its 2nd-gen autonomy kit; Caterpillar's five-machine autonomous construction lineup remains in early rollout. Both stay diversified industrials where robotics is a small slice of the overall return — no fresh sector-specific news this week.
JAPAN FA
Japanese factory automation
Fanuc has given back some of its post-crash bounce, trading near ¥6,063 as of Aug 19 versus the ¥6,720 level seen the prior week — a reminder the recovery from the Aug 3 guidance shock is still choppy rather than a clean re-rating. Harmonic Drive Systems, a key humanoid-actuator supplier, sits at ¥5,910 (Aug 28) against a 7-buy/1-sell analyst consensus target of ¥7,731.
COMPONENTS
Supply chain & components
MP Materials extended its rally from $38 (Jul 29) to $60.05 (Aug 21) on a new gadolinium supply deal with a US aerospace/defense customer and accelerating 10X magnet-facility buildout; Morgan Stanley raised its price target to $73 (Overweight). A newer name worth tracking: Vishay Precision Group is now 2026's best-performing robotics supply-chain stock (+216% YTD, ahead of Ouster and Aeva), booking real humanoid-actuator orders on its strain gauges and load cells.
AI COMPUTE
The robot "brain"
NVIDIA's Jetson Thor platform reached general availability (developer kit and production modules) on Aug 10, and the company's Aug 26 Q2 FY27 report showed why the compute layer still dominates the conversation: revenue of $96.22B (+5.7% above guidance), Data Center at $89B, and Q3 guidance of $108B. Adopters now span Agility, Amazon Robotics, Boston Dynamics, Caterpillar, Figure, Hexagon, Medtronic, Meta and China's Galbot.
CHINA
China scaling & listing
Unitree's post-debut crash is now the sector's central China story — a ~45% slump inside a week that has reopened the "is this a bubble" debate for the whole 150+-company field. Elsewhere, Agibot says cumulative humanoid shipments have reached 15,000 units (up from 10,000 just weeks ago), and LimX Dynamics continues scaling its COSA operating system ahead of a planned Hong Kong listing.

05 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Unitree's crash is the bubble case made real
Shares fell ~45% from their Aug 19 intraday peak to the Aug 24-28 close, erasing roughly RMB 200B in days. Bank J. Safra Sarasin's Asia CIO called the valuation "probably not justified," citing an "infant level" industry with intensifying competition. This is no longer a hypothetical bubble warning — it's a live repricing investors can watch happen.
Humanoid funding still outruns humanoid revenue
The median disclosed humanoid round between Aug 2025 and June 2026 was $145M, with rounds above $50M now 84% of all disclosed capital — most of these companies still don't have revenue that justifies the price tag. South Korea's Tomorrow Robotics went from ~30B won to a 450B won target valuation in under a year without a single confirmed commercial deployment.
Demos ≠ autonomy
If a humanoid demo isn't explicitly labeled autonomous, assume teleoperation. Dexterity, inference speed, reliability, and cost still confine real use to structured factory/warehouse tasks.
Crowding & shakeout risk
150+ companies chasing humanoids, with Agibot alone now claiming 15,000 cumulative units shipped; a reshuffling of startups is widely expected. Robotics pioneer Rodney Brooks argues much of the investment will be wasted.
Backlogs and beats don't protect the stock
Symbotic got a fresh Goldman price-target cut (to $45 from $54) and an insider share sale this week — on top of last week's dilutive offering — despite real revenue growth. AeroVironment and Kratos both won new contracts this month, yet their shares have traded down in 2026 anyway. In parts of this sector, balance-sheet and sentiment signals are doing more work than headline numbers.
Revenue is elsewhere
Industrial, logistics, and surgical robots generate proven revenue today; humanoids largely don't yet. Component and warehouse names carry less thesis risk than humanoid pure-plays.
China price war
Unitree's $5,900 R1 signals aggressive price compression that could squeeze Western OEM margins before the market matures — and 150+ Chinese humanoid startups, now including BYD, are competing on price and scale even as Unitree's own stock struggles to hold its valuation.
"Dot-com stage" warnings
KB Securities likened 2026's robotics/space enthusiasm to the final stage of the dot-com bubble; Ray Dalio separately compared the broader AI bubble to 1929 and 2000 valuation extremes. Sector EV/EBITDA multiples ranged from 0.9x (mature industrials) to 78.2x (speculative pure-plays) in Q4 2025 — a valuation split investors should not treat as one trade.
