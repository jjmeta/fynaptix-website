05 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Bubble warnings from serious sources
China's NDRC has warned of a humanoid "bubble" — capital flooding in while verified applications remain scarce. Morgan Stanley flags a wide gap between hype and scalability; some VCs compare the setup to the dotcom era.
Demos ≠ autonomy
If a humanoid demo isn't explicitly labeled autonomous, assume teleoperation. Dexterity, inference speed, reliability, and cost still confine real use to structured factory/warehouse tasks.
Crowding & shakeout risk
150+ companies chasing humanoids; a reshuffling of startups is widely expected. Robotics pioneer Rodney Brooks argues much of the investment will be wasted.
Fundamentals already diverging
Unitree's Q1 2026 profit fell 52% YoY even as its IPO was approved; ISRG fell 25%+ despite beating earnings; drone stocks fell despite record backlogs. Multiples, not fundamentals, are doing the work in parts of the sector.
Revenue is elsewhere
Industrial, logistics, and surgical robots generate proven revenue today; humanoids largely don't yet. Component and warehouse names carry less thesis risk than humanoid pure-plays.
China price war
Unitree's $5,900 R1 signals aggressive price compression that could squeeze Western OEM margins before the market matures.
