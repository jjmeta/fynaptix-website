04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Biology still fails in the clinic
AI speeds discovery, but most drugs still fail in humans; one Phase IIa success is not a field-wide validation.
Catalyst-driven volatility
These stocks trade on announcements; between catalysts they drift and dilute.
Platform-value skepticism
If pharma can license AI tools cheaply (or build in-house), platform companies may capture less value than valuations imply.
Rate sensitivity
Long-duration biotech cash flows are highly sensitive to rates; macro can swamp fundamentals.
