04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Biology still fails in the clinic
Of 173 AI-discovered programs now in clinical development, zero have reached full FDA approval; Recursion fell ~7.9% on a single unfavorable readout on Jul 16, 2026 — a reminder that one Phase IIa success is not field-wide validation.
Catalyst-driven volatility
These stocks trade on announcements; between catalysts they drift and dilute. Relay (RLAY) collected both fresh Buy initiations and a Zacks downgrade to Strong Sell within the same two weeks in July. Generate (GENB) and Eikon (EIKN) both traded below their Feb 2026 IPO prices shortly after debut.
Platform-value skepticism
If pharma can license AI tools cheaply (or build in-house), platform companies may capture less value than valuations imply. The broader AI trade faces its own version of this: hyperscalers' ~$660–690B in planned 2026 infrastructure spend dwarfs the AI software revenue it's meant to support.
Rate sensitivity
Long-duration biotech cash flows are highly sensitive to rates; macro can swamp fundamentals regardless of platform progress.
