01 // THE THESIS
What Lightspeed Venture Partners Actually Believes
FOUNDING BELIEF
Great companies are built everywhere
Lightspeed runs separate funds across the US, Europe, India, Israel and Southeast Asia — all LP'd into the same network. This geographic thesis is now vindicated: their India fund has produced returns that rival their US early-stage record.
ENTERPRISE DEPTH
Infrastructure and developer tools as a core practice
Lightspeed has backed every major enterprise wave: SaaS transition (Mulesoft, Nutanix), cloud security (Rubrik), and now AI infrastructure. Their enterprise partners bring direct operating experience from engineering leadership roles.
CONSUMER CONVICTION
They backed Snap before the Series A
Jeremy Liew found Snapchat through a blog post, invested at seed, and held through the IPO. Lightspeed has a track record of finding consumer-social companies early when growth signals are there but monetisation is not yet obvious.
GAMING THESIS
Games are the leading edge of immersive computing
The investment in Epic Games reflects a thesis that gaming engines, virtual worlds and creator economies are the first form of immersive computing. Their gaming practice connects to AR/VR infrastructure and social computing.
GLOBAL ARBITRAGE
US frameworks applied globally create 10x opportunities
Lightspeed's India fund repeatedly found companies running US playbooks in markets with 10x lower customer acquisition costs. This pattern-matching — 'what just worked in the US will work here in 3 years' — has been one of their most consistent alpha sources.
PATIENCE ON ENTERPRISE
Enterprise software takes longer than people think
Lightspeed held AppDynamics through years of slow enterprise sales cycles before Cisco acquired it on the eve of the IPO. Their enterprise approach is patient capital that understands institutional procurement dynamics.
Key Partners: Barry Eggers · Ravi Mhatre · Jeremy Liew (Consumer) · Gaurav Gupta (Enterprise) · Mercedes Bent

05 // HOW TO READ LIGHTSPEED VENTURE PARTNERS
Strengths, Blind Spots & the Honest Assessment
Consumer instincts are genuinely rare
Finding Snapchat at seed, Epic Games at growth — and being right about both — requires a specific skill for reading consumer behaviour ahead of monetisation. Their consumer track record is one of the best in the industry.
Enterprise depth is under-appreciated
Mulesoft, AppDynamics and Rubrik are enormous outcomes. The pattern — patient enterprise build, acquisition at the right moment — has repeated multiple times.
The global strategy adds complexity
Running five separate geographic funds with different teams and different LPs creates coordination costs. Not all LPs want global diversification.
Consumer social is harder now
Snapchat is competing with TikTok, Instagram Reels and BeReal simultaneously. The consumer social landscape is harder to invest in now than during the 2010s mobile wave.
How to use Lightspeed research
Their enterprise content — on infrastructure, security, developer tooling — is a useful check against your sector dashboards. The gaming content reflects a coherent immersive computing thesis worth tracking.
