What They're Saying
Recent public commentary and moves, most consequential first. Refreshed every Monday. Cards marked “carried over” had no new verified material this week.
The backdrop NO SINGLE VOICE NEUTRAL (CONTEXT FOR EVERYTHING BELOW)
NOV 5–6, 2026
The Nasdaq Composite fell 5.52% on Thursday Nov 5, its worst day in 19 months; the S&P 500 fell 4.36% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index about 12%. One quoted view, from an unnamed Jefferies strategist as relayed by Reuters via a blog: the selloff was “reactionary, not a fundamental reset.”
Per secondary reports, the triggers were Democratic wins in the midterms with pledges to tighten data-centre rules (Goldman reportedly estimates about 45% of planned US capacity expansion could be delayed; UBS about 30% of 2028 additions), a weak Qualcomm outlook, and doubts about AI returns. Nvidia fell 9.26% to $181.60, AMD about 17%, and the Nasdaq closed roughly 12% below its Oct 6 record. Against that, AMD’s Q3 data-centre revenue rose 97% and Microsoft’s Azure 48%, while Amazon’s trailing free cash flow reportedly swung to a $42.4bn outflow (figures vary between the outlets, so treat them as approximate). Every card below was written into this tape. Relevant to AI & Compute and Macro.

Cathie Wood ARK INVEST BULL (BUYING THE SELLOFF)
NOV 5–6, 2026
“The more the market worries, the more optimistic I get, because that often means the bull market is more durable.” And: “We’ve added to our SpaceX position.” (CNBC interview, Nov 6, as reported by BiGGo; other outlets word the first line slightly differently, e.g. “I always feel better when the market is worried.”)
On Nov 5, per ARK’s daily trade file as reported by TheStreet, ARK bought 252,592 Nvidia shares (about $47m at $186.10) the day Nvidia fell 9.26%, also adding AMD, SpaceX, Alphabet and others while trimming CoreWeave, Broadcom and TSMC. Wood attributed part of the correction to the bond market and said she does not think the bull market has “gone off the rails.” The scorecard matters: TheStreet puts ARKK at −3.12% year to date against +7.81% for the S&P 500 (last week’s edition cited +11.05%), −9.62% annualised over five years against +9.38%, with about $2.2bn of net outflows in the month to Nov 4 (VettaFi). Buying dips has worked for ARK in past recoveries and badly in 2021–22. Relevant to ARK Invest, Space and AI & Compute.

Greg Abel / Warren Buffett BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY NEUTRAL (CASH-RICH, BUYING BACK STOCK)
NOV 7, 2026 (Q3 RESULTS)
Action, not a quote: Berkshire repurchased a record-for-the-Abel-era $9.6bn of its own stock in Q3, about $2bn more in the first four weeks of October, and ended September with $356.9bn of cash and equivalents. Reuters quotes only a company statement, that “considerable uncertainty” remains about economic and geopolitical outcomes.
Operating profit fell 6% to $12.71bn, with insurance profit down 26% and GEICO pretax profit down 45%. Forbes (partly summarised in my fetch, so unconfirmed) reports a net sale of roughly $426m of stocks in the quarter; the 13F due Nov 16 will show the detail. Buying back your own shares while the market fell is a statement about price, not a call on AI, and a patient cash pile earned nothing in a rally. No direct Abel quote was verified. Relevant to AI & Compute and Macro.

Michael Burry CASSANDRA UNCHAINED (FORMERLY SCION) BEAR (TOOK SOME PROFIT, STILL SHORT)
NOV 5–6, 2026 (REPORTED)
Action, not a quote: per 24/7 Wall St., Burry told subscribers (a paywalled “Trading Post” dated Nov 5) that he sold 25% of his SMH semiconductor-ETF puts after SMH fell 11.21% to $447.73, and kept most of the position. No verbatim Burry quote was available to me.
The claim is second-hand: the post is paywalled, there are no strikes or profit figures, and no filing confirms it. The same report says his bearish exposure has been concentrated in Nasdaq-100 and SMH puts, with the SMH puts expiring June 2027. Taking profit on a quarter after a one-day crash can be read as discipline or as a lack of conviction in further falls; he was early on this trade for a long time, and one violent day does not settle whether the AI build-out is a bubble. Earlier reports of his Palantir puts are not updated here. Relevant to AI & Compute.

Masayoshi Son SOFTBANK GROUP BULL (UNDER MARKET PRESSURE)
NOV 6, 2026
Action, not a quote: SoftBank shares fell about 11% on Friday Nov 6 amid the Asian chip rout, cutting Son’s paper wealth by roughly $3.9bn to about $29bn (Bloomberg wealth data, as relayed by Billionaires.Africa). No new Son statement was verified this week.
Reuters, per the same outlet, reported SoftBank’s bond default-insurance cost at a four-year high. As background from an August preview (not this week): more than $60bn committed to OpenAI and related projects, a roughly $22.5bn OpenAI tranche due by year-end, and a $40bn bridge loan and $20bn Arm-backed margin loan on the books. Leverage magnifies both outcomes: it is what let Son hold the biggest OpenAI stake, and what makes a falling share price dangerous. Relevant to AI & Compute and Robotics.

04 // HOW TO USE THIS
Reading Famous Investors Without Getting Hurt
Survivorship bias is enormous
You hear from the ones who were right. The equally confident managers who blew up don't get interviewed. Fame is evidence of a good run, not of a repeatable process.
Their constraints aren't yours
Burry can hold puts that expire worthless; Berkshire can sit in cash for years; Son can lose $10bn and continue. Your timeline, tax position and tolerance are different — copying their positions without their balance sheet is how people get hurt.
13F filings lag by up to 45 days
Anything sourced from filings shows long US equity positions only — no shorts, bonds, derivatives or overseas holdings — and may already have been sold. Treat it as archaeology, not a signal.
BETTER USE
Mine the reasoning, not the ticker
The valuable part is why — Burry's depreciation argument, Ackman's disruption-risk framing, Wood's convergence thesis. Test those arguments against your own sector pages, then log where you land in the Decision Journal.
BEST USE
Find who disagrees with you
If everyone on this page you find persuasive sits on the same side of the spectrum, that's a warning about your reading habits rather than a confirmation of your thesis.
