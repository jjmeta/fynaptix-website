What They're Saying

Cathie Wood ARK INVEST BULL (BUYING THE DIP)
SEP 4–5, 2026
Added Robinhood and a Solana staking ETF the same day the Nasdaq fell roughly 4% on hot jobs data — while trimming Tempus AI and Twist Bioscience.
Paraphrased from ARK's daily trade disclosures. A stronger-than-expected August jobs report (162,000 vs. 55,000 expected) revived Fed rate-hike fears on Friday, Sept. 4, and momentum names across chips, AI infrastructure and quantum computing sold off hard. Wood's response was to buy directly into the weakness — adding roughly $3.6M of Robinhood after a 17% weekly surge and building a new Solana ETF stake — rather than retreat to cash. It's the clearest test yet of her "innovation compounds through drawdowns" playbook against a macro-driven, not thesis-driven, selloff. Relevant to your AI & Compute page.

Michael Burry CASSANDRA UNCHAINED (FORMERLY SCION) BEAR (ESCALATING)
AUG 10 – SEP 2, 2026
Refreshed his Palantir short with March 2027 puts struck in the low-to-mid $100s after the stock ripped 64% off its June low — reiterating a long-term fair value "under $1."
Burry had partially covered his short near $107 before Palantir surged back to $175; rather than stay out, he rebuilt the position at cheaper implied volatility on Aug. 10, and by Sept. 2 was calling the company "a consultant riding a bubble of AI FOMO demand." The week's broader momentum-stock selloff — Micron, Marvell, AMD and the AI-infrastructure names all fell hard on Sept. 4 — is the first real market move in his favor since he re-shorted, though Palantir itself has repeatedly shrugged off his calls before. Directly relevant to your AI & Compute page.

Ray Dalio BRIDGEWATER (FOUNDER) BEAR (MACRO)
SEP 4, 2026
"I fear that short-term rates and the dollar will go down, especially relative to gold, long-term interest rates will rise a bit... and stocks will do relatively poorly despite the easing."
Said during a Reddit AMA, Dalio's argument is that a Fed rate cut wouldn't be the relief rally many are pricing in, because it would signal the Fed is "in a bind," push investors toward gold, and raise the odds of stagflation. The irony: the market move that actually happened this week ran the other way — stocks fell because a hot jobs report made a cut look less likely, not because one arrived. His mechanism hasn't been tested yet, even though his general caution has, so far, been directionally right about volatility. Relevant to your Macro page.

Philippe Laffont COATUE MANAGEMENT BULL (NEW 13F)
Q2 13F, ANALYZED EARLY SEP 2026
Took a $3.17bn stake in SpaceX (6.52% of the portfolio) while increasing Micron 18-fold and Amazon 49% — and exited Visa and Chime entirely.
Paraphrased from Coatue's newly-parsed Q2 filing. The through-line is AI infrastructure end to end: Micron supplies the high-bandwidth memory the systems consume, Amazon turns that capacity into cloud services, and SpaceX is Laffont's bet that satellite and launch infrastructure is the next scarce layer. Dropping two consumer-finance names (Visa, Chime) to fund it is as clear a statement of conviction as a 13F gets. Relevant to your Space Economy and AI & Compute pages.

04 // HOW TO USE THIS
Reading Famous Investors Without Getting Hurt
Survivorship bias is enormous
You hear from the ones who were right. The equally confident managers who blew up don't get interviewed. Fame is evidence of a good run, not of a repeatable process.
Their constraints aren't yours
Burry can hold puts that expire worthless; Berkshire can sit in cash for years; Son can lose $10bn and continue. Your timeline, tax position and tolerance are different — copying their positions without their balance sheet is how people get hurt.
13F filings lag by up to 45 days
Anything sourced from filings shows long US equity positions only — no shorts, bonds, derivatives or overseas holdings — and may already have been sold. Treat it as archaeology, not a signal.
BETTER USE
Mine the reasoning, not the ticker
The valuable part is why — Gurley's funding argument, Buffett's price discipline, Son's convergence thesis. Test those arguments against your own sector pages, then log where you land in the Decision Journal.
BEST USE
Find who disagrees with you
If everyone on this page you find persuasive sits on the same side of the spectrum, that's a warning about your reading habits rather than a confirmation of your thesis.
