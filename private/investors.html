02 // THIS WEEK
What They're Saying
Recent public commentary and moves, most consequential first. Refreshed every Monday. Cards marked “carried over” had no new verified material this week.
Ken Griffin CITADEL CAUTIOUS (BUBBLES “WITHIN” THE MOMENT, STILL OWNS THE NAMES)
OCT 20, 2026 (REPORTED OCT 22 & 28)
“There’s almost certainly bubbles within this moment in time.” (as quoted by 24/7 Wall St., which attributes it to Griffin’s Bloomberg interview at Citadel’s Future of Global Markets Conference; I did not verify against Bloomberg itself.)
Per the same report he compared GPUs to airline seats, whose prices can fall once capacity exceeds demand, likened the moment to the ~2000 fibre-optic build-out, and questioned whether businesses extract enough value from costly models, while saying AI agents cut some Citadel research tasks from weeks to hours (all paraphrase). The “money” side: Citadel Advisors’ 13F for June 30 (secondary summary, not checked on EDGAR) shows Amazon ~$2.59bn, Nvidia ~$2.36bn, Microsoft ~$1.41bn, with Nvidia shares down ~37% and Microsoft up ~43% on the quarter, plus unsized call and put options on Nvidia and Micron. A June snapshot says nothing about today, and a market-maker’s book is not a directional bet. A separate Motley Fool piece reduces his remark to “almost certainly” in a bubble; that is a compression, not his words. Relevant to AI & Compute and Macro.

Michael Burry CASSANDRA UNCHAINED (FORMERLY SCION) BEAR (PALANTIR PUTS, HELD)
REPORTED NOV 1, 2026 (SUBSTACK POST UNDATED)
“I now own the [Palantir] June 17, 2027 Strike Price 50 Puts and the December 18, 2026 Strike Price 100 Puts. I am not selling these today.” And: “I continue to hold the puts, as I believe the fundamental value of this company is well under $50/share.”
Quoted by a general-news outlet from Burry’s Substack; the post is neither linked nor dated, the bracketed “[Palantir]” is the outlet’s insertion, and contract counts and cost were not given, so treat the detail as second-hand. The article says he held on after an ~18% three-day drop and a presidential endorsement of Palantir’s military platform lifted the stock; Palantir’s Q3 results were due Nov 2. The calls carry a clear scorecard: ARK trimmed Palantir into strength last month, and Burry’s Palantir and Nvidia bearishness has been early for long stretches. Puts expiring Dec 2026 need a fall below $100 soon; the 2027 puts have more time. Relevant to AI & Compute.

04 // HOW TO USE THIS
Reading Famous Investors Without Getting Hurt
Survivorship bias is enormous
You hear from the ones who were right. The equally confident managers who blew up don't get interviewed. Fame is evidence of a good run, not of a repeatable process.
Their constraints aren't yours
Burry can hold puts that expire worthless; Berkshire can sit in cash for years; Son can lose $10bn and continue. Your timeline, tax position and tolerance are different — copying their positions without their balance sheet is how people get hurt.
13F filings lag by up to 45 days
Anything sourced from filings shows long US equity positions only — no shorts, bonds, derivatives or overseas holdings — and may already have been sold. Treat it as archaeology, not a signal.
BETTER USE
Mine the reasoning, not the ticker
The valuable part is why — Burry's depreciation argument, Ackman's disruption-risk framing, Wood's convergence thesis. Test those arguments against your own sector pages, then log where you land in the Decision Journal.
BEST USE
Find who disagrees with you
If everyone on this page you find persuasive sits on the same side of the spectrum, that's a warning about your reading habits rather than a confirmation of your thesis.
