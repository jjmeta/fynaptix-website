Ray Dalio BRIDGEWATER (FOUNDER) BEAR (MACRO, CONCENTRATION RISK)
OCT 21, 2026 (POST ON X)
“Pay attention to the concentration that is existing in one thing right now: AI.” And: “I want to have less concentration on one thing and more diversification right now.”
Quoted from Dalio’s Oct 21 X post as reported by Gokhshtein; he also told followers to watch the bond market and the cost of money, “the backbone to everything.” This is softer and more general than his Oct 14 interview (reported last week) putting his bubble indicator at 1929/2000 scale, and it describes his own posture rather than a dated forecast. Bubble calls without a trigger date have a long record of being early, and his indicator’s inputs remain undisclosed. Relevant to your AI & Compute and Macro pages.

Cathie Wood ARK INVEST BULL (BUYING DIPS, TRIMMING STRENGTH)
OCT 15–22, 2026 (TRADES)
Action, not a quote: on Oct 22, the day after Meta’s earnings sent the stock down 7%, ARK funds bought 38,110 Meta shares (about $24.5m). Over Oct 15–21 they sold 79,290 Palantir shares (about $16m).
TheStreet reports Wood praised Meta’s third-quarter stats on X; Meta beat on revenue ($64.6bn) but missed on EPS after a $9.89bn settlement charge and lifted the low end of 2026 capex to $135–145bn, exactly the AI-spending worry bears cite. On Oct 21 ARK also bought Oklo, X-Energy and Intuitive Machines. The scorecard matters: TheStreet puts ARKK up 11.05% year to date versus 13.32% for the S&P 500, and down 6.36% annualised over five years versus +11.26%, with about $1.64bn of net outflows over twelve months. Buying weakness is her long-standing pattern; whether it is conviction or a habit that has cost investors is the live question. Relevant to your AI & Compute, Nuclear, Space and ARK Invest pages.

04 // HOW TO USE THIS
Reading Famous Investors Without Getting Hurt
Survivorship bias is enormous
You hear from the ones who were right. The equally confident managers who blew up don't get interviewed. Fame is evidence of a good run, not of a repeatable process.
Their constraints aren't yours
Burry can hold puts that expire worthless; Berkshire can sit in cash for years; Son can lose $10bn and continue. Your timeline, tax position and tolerance are different — copying their positions without their balance sheet is how people get hurt.
13F filings lag by up to 45 days
Anything sourced from filings shows long US equity positions only — no shorts, bonds, derivatives or overseas holdings — and may already have been sold. Treat it as archaeology, not a signal.
BETTER USE
Mine the reasoning, not the ticker
The valuable part is why — Burry's depreciation argument, Ackman's disruption-risk framing, Wood's convergence thesis. Test those arguments against your own sector pages, then log where you land in the Decision Journal.
BEST USE
Find who disagrees with you
If everyone on this page you find persuasive sits on the same side of the spectrum, that's a warning about your reading habits rather than a confirmation of your thesis.
