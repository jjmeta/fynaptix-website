Michael Burry CASSANDRA UNCHAINED (FORMERLY SCION) BEAR (ESCALATING)
AUG 26, 2026
Nvidia's new $500bn AI financing platform is a "Wall Street stunt" with "shades of Enron."
Burry crossed a line this week: for the first time he added an outright short on Nvidia itself, on top of existing shorts in Oracle, Palantir, Nebius and Caterpillar — he says his total short book is now over 21% of his portfolio, excluding puts. He also bought December Nvidia calls as an explicit "hedge" against his own short ("I am not playing for gains here"), and argues Nvidia's push to unlock $500bn in financing — where Nvidia takes equity stakes and offers residual-value guarantees on its own chip sales — recreates the debt structures that fueled 2008, "filtered through Private Equity's Private Credit schemes." His theoretical value for Nvidia, he says, is "much lower than today's market value." Directly relevant to your AI & Compute page.

Pershing Square's biggest portfolio overhaul in years — new stakes in Netflix, Visa, Mastercard, Alcon, Intercontinental Exchange and S&P Global.

Treasury's debt buyback plan, paired with Japan trimming its holdings of U.S. bonds, is a sign "a debt crisis is getting closer."
Paraphrased from Dalio's CNBC remarks. 04 // HOW TO USE THIS
Reading Famous Investors Without Getting Hurt
Survivorship bias is enormous
You hear from the ones who were right. The equally confident managers who blew up don't get interviewed. Fame is evidence of a good run, not of a repeatable process.
Their constraints aren't yours
Burry can hold puts that expire worthless; Berkshire can sit in cash for years; Son can lose $10bn and continue. Your timeline, tax position and tolerance are different — copying their positions without their balance sheet is how people get hurt.
13F filings lag by up to 45 days
Anything sourced from filings shows long US equity positions only — no shorts, bonds, derivatives or overseas holdings — and may already have been sold. Treat it as archaeology, not a signal.
BETTER USE
Mine the reasoning, not the ticker
The valuable part is why — Gurley's funding argument, Buffett's price discipline, Son's convergence thesis. Test those arguments against your own sector pages, then log where you land in the Decision Journal.
BEST USE
Find who disagrees with you
If everyone on this page you find persuasive sits on the same side of the spectrum, that's a warning about your reading habits rather than a confirmation of your thesis.
