02 // WHY EACH MATTERS
Reading the Backdrop
RATES (US10Y)
The gravity on growth stocks
Rising long yields compress valuations of long-duration cash flows — quantum, humanoids, pre-revenue SMRs, and biotech feel it first and hardest.
VIX
Risk appetite gauge
Sustained VIX spikes drain speculative sectors regardless of fundamentals; also historically the best moments to deploy the cash "opportunity" sleeve.
GOLD
Hedge confirmation
Gold strength alongside equity weakness is your diversification working; gold + stocks rising together often signals inflation/currency concerns.
BITCOIN
Liquidity thermometer
BTC tends to front-run liquidity shifts — weakness there often precedes pressure on the most speculative equity sleeves.
DOLLAR (DXY)
Overseas earnings & commodities
A strong dollar pressures commodity prices (minerals) and international revenue (Japanese FA names); a weak dollar is a tailwind for both.
03 // CURRENT READ
This Week's Commentary
A regime shift, not just a shock, defined this week. The Fed delivered its first rate hike since 2023 on Sept 16 — a unanimous 12-0 vote lifting the federal funds rate 25bp to 3.75%-4% — with Chair Warsh saying inflation has run "too high... for too long," a direct consequence of the Iran-driven oil spike compounding an already-firm August payrolls report (+162K vs. a 52K estimate). The 10-year spiked as high as ~5.01% on Sept 18, briefly nearing the 2007 record of 5.04%, before easing back toward ~4.94-4.95% by week's end — the AI buildout's own debt-funding wave (~$300B of AI-linked investment-grade issuance projected for 2026) is compounding the Fed's move, not just replacing it. Oddly, the VIX actually eased to ~14.81 from last week's 18.05 spike as the decision itself resolved uncertainty; gold held its safe-haven bid near $4,383/oz (+0.97% on the day) and bitcoin kept recovering, back to the high-$70,000s from the mid-$60,000s a few weeks ago, while the dollar index firmed modestly to ~99.94. Markets now price at least one more 25bp hike before year-end, with most officials eyeing 4.1-4.4% by December — a genuinely higher-for-longer backdrop for every long-duration name on this hub. Beneath the rate shock, the stance board saw its heaviest week of changes yet: Oracle, BAE Systems, AeroVironment, Centrus Energy, Energy Fuels, Redwire, Illumina, Intellia, Microsoft, IonQ, Rigetti and Quantinuum were all raised on converting contracts, regulatory de-risking or partner-validated technical milestones, while Kratos, Red Cat, L3Harris, Planet Labs, Fluence Energy, Stem and NVIDIA's quantum-sleeve position were all trimmed — mostly on missed proof points rather than any single scandal. The starkest single data point: Holtec Nuclear pulled its ~$900M Nasdaq IPO on Sept 16-18 citing "market conditions" days after targeting a $10.2B valuation — a real operating company with $564M of trailing revenue stepping back is the clearest sign yet that 2026's most speculative corners are starting to get repriced. For the week ahead: Xi Jinping's Sept 24-25 Washington visit is the dominant catalyst, with rare-earth export licensing squarely on the agenda 47 days ahead of the Nov 10 export-control deadline, alongside Micron's Sept 25 earnings and a heavy slate of Fed speakers parsing what comes next. — Updated Sep 21, 2026; refreshed weekly.
