Reading the backdrop

Rates (US10Y) The gravity on growth stocks Rising long yields compress valuations of long-duration cash flows — quantum, humanoids, pre-revenue SMRs and biotech feel it first and hardest.
VIX Risk appetite gauge Sustained spikes drain speculative sectors regardless of fundamentals — also historically the best moments to deploy the cash "opportunity" sleeve.
Gold Hedge confirmation Gold strength alongside equity weakness is diversification working; gold and stocks rising together often signals inflation or currency concerns.
Bitcoin Liquidity thermometer BTC tends to front-run liquidity shifts — weakness there often precedes pressure on the most speculative equity sleeves.
Dollar (DXY) Overseas earnings & commodities A strong dollar pressures commodity prices (minerals) and international revenue (Japanese FA names); a weak dollar is a tailwind for both.

This week's commentary

A soft September jobs report took the edge off the rate squeeze: payrolls rose just 29,000 against ~84,000 expected, unemployment ticked up to 4.2%, and CME FedWatch odds of another hike this month collapsed to roughly 14% from ~70% earlier in the week. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 5.18% — still near its highest levels since 2007 — with the Fed funds range at 3.75–4.00% following the Sept 16 hike.

Equities took the data as a green light: the S&P 500 closed Friday near 7,666 and the Nasdaq near 26,872, while the VIX slipped to about 15.6. A weak labor market is a mixed signal rather than a clean positive, though — it lowers the odds of further tightening but raises the question of whether growth is rolling over just as AI-linked debt issuance keeps adding supply.

Beneath the tape, the stance board moved only toward caution: Oracle was cut to Avoid after the BIS and RBA published the first official measures of AI circular financing (55.2% of AI-firm investment funded by other AI firms; US$1–1.5T of off-balance-sheet hyperscaler obligations), Teradyne and Quantinuum were cut to Watch on stretched valuations, and Critical Metals was added at Avoid. There were no upgrades — a week where good news was already in the price.

For the week ahead the data calendar is light: FOMC minutes on Oct 7 give the first detailed look at the September hike debate, Treasury auctions test appetite for duration after the jobs-driven rally, and jobless claims and consumer sentiment close the week before Q3 earnings season accelerates. Further out, Anthropic’s reported roadshow the week of Nov 9 and China’s rare-earth export reprieve expiring Nov 10 are the policy-driven dates that matter most for the speculative end of this hub. Bitcoin and ether levels were not re-verified this week.
