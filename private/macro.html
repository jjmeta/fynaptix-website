02 // WHY EACH MATTERS
Reading the Backdrop
RATES (US10Y)
The gravity on growth stocks
Rising long yields compress valuations of long-duration cash flows — quantum, humanoids, pre-revenue SMRs, and biotech feel it first and hardest.
VIX
Risk appetite gauge
Sustained VIX spikes drain speculative sectors regardless of fundamentals; also historically the best moments to deploy the cash "opportunity" sleeve.
GOLD
Hedge confirmation
Gold strength alongside equity weakness is your diversification working; gold + stocks rising together often signals inflation/currency concerns.
BITCOIN
Liquidity thermometer
BTC tends to front-run liquidity shifts — weakness there often precedes pressure on the most speculative equity sleeves.
DOLLAR (DXY)
Overseas earnings & commodities
A strong dollar pressures commodity prices (minerals) and international revenue (Japanese FA names); a weak dollar is a tailwind for both.
03 // CURRENT READ
This Week's Commentary
The calm index-level tape of recent weeks broke this week. Reignited conflict around Iran and renewed Strait of Hormuz tanker disruption sent oil sharply higher, and the S&P 500 and Nasdaq headed into today's session down roughly 1.6% week-to-date on a fourth straight losing day — the VIX jumped from ~14.5 to 18.05 (+9.67%) as risk appetite reset. The 10-year sits around 4.22-4.24%, little changed on the week, while the dollar index firmed slightly to ~98.97. Gold caught a fresh safe-haven bid to roughly $4,335-4,400/oz on the geopolitical shock, and bitcoin has actually recovered into the high-$70,000s from the mid-$60,000s a few weeks ago — a reminder that the "risk-off" read isn't uniform across assets this time. Today's August CPI report is the last major data point before the Fed's Sept 15-16 meeting, and oil's spike has genuinely complicated the picture: futures have swung toward meaningful hike risk rather than the cut path priced earlier this year, a hawkish surprise given August payrolls actually came in strong (+162K vs. a 52K estimate, unemployment steady at 4.1%). Underneath the macro shock, the same bifurcation held in single names: Alphabet, MP Materials, Symbotic, Red Cat, X-energy, Redwire, AST SpaceMobile, Energy Vault, 908 Devices, NVIDIA and IonQ were all raised this week on converting backlogs or partner-validated data, while Unitree, AeroVironment, Oklo, Albemarle, Rocket Lab, D-Wave (QBTS) and Pasqal (PSQL) were all cut on dilution, goodwill impairments, delayed milestones or a post-debut price break. For the week ahead: the Sept 15-16 FOMC decision is the dominant catalyst, alongside Starship's Sept 15 orbital attempt and Xi Jinping's Sept 24 Washington visit, where rare-earth export access is squarely on the agenda. The throughline hasn't changed even with the macro shock layered on top: markets are still rewarding capex and backlogs tied to visible, converting growth and punishing capex or milestones without it — oil and rates just became the more urgent filter this week. — Updated Sep 11, 2026; refreshed weekly.
