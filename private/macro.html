02 // WHY EACH MATTERS
Reading the Backdrop
RATES (US10Y)
The gravity on growth stocks
Rising long yields compress valuations of long-duration cash flows — quantum, humanoids, pre-revenue SMRs, and biotech feel it first and hardest.
VIX
Risk appetite gauge
Sustained VIX spikes drain speculative sectors regardless of fundamentals; also historically the best moments to deploy the cash "opportunity" sleeve.
GOLD
Hedge confirmation
Gold strength alongside equity weakness is your diversification working; gold + stocks rising together often signals inflation/currency concerns.
BITCOIN
Liquidity thermometer
BTC tends to front-run liquidity shifts — weakness there often precedes pressure on the most speculative equity sleeves.
DOLLAR (DXY)
Overseas earnings & commodities
A strong dollar pressures commodity prices (minerals) and international revenue (Japanese FA names); a weak dollar is a tailwind for both.
03 // CURRENT READ
This Week's Commentary
The higher-for-longer regime the Fed set in motion on Sept 16 is still the backdrop, but this week was about AI's own leaders questioning the pace of the buildout they created. The 10-year touched ~5.01% on Sept 18, briefly nearing the 2007 record of 5.04%, before easing to ~4.94-4.95% — the AI sector's own debt-funding wave (an estimated ~$300B of AI-linked investment-grade issuance for 2026) is compounding the Fed's move, not just replacing it, with BofA pegging roughly 0.3 percentage points of the rise on that issuance alone. The VIX eased to ~14.81 (-4.08% on Sept 18) as post-hike uncertainty resolved; gold held its safe-haven bid near $4,383/oz (+0.97%) and bitcoin continued recovering toward the high-$70,000s, while the dollar index firmed to ~100.20. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's Sept 12 essay calling for the industry to "pace the frontier" — backed publicly by OpenAI's Sam Altman and DeepMind's Demis Hassabis — sent chip names lower for several sessions while Meta, Alphabet and Microsoft rose, a genuine sentiment move on top of the rate story. Beneath it, the stance board saw one of its heaviest weeks of changes yet, 24 in total: Vishay Precision Group and J&J's Ottava (Robotics), BAE Systems and AeroVironment (Defense), Centrus Energy (Nuclear), Energy Fuels (Minerals), Rocket Lab, L3Harris, ASTS and Intuitive Machines (Space), Eos Energy (Storage), Illumina, Intellia and Quantum-Si (Biotech), and IBM, NVIDIA and IonQ (Quantum) were all raised on converting contracts, regulatory de-risking or partner-validated technical milestones, while Kratos and Red Cat (Defense, the latter to Avoid), Redwire and Planet Labs (Space), and Alphabet, Rigetti and Quantinuum (Quantum) were trimmed — mostly on missed proof points rather than any single scandal. The starkest single data point: Holtec Nuclear pulled its ~$900M Nasdaq IPO on Sept 16-18 citing "market conditions" days after targeting a $10.2B valuation — a real operating company with $564M of trailing revenue stepping back is the clearest sign yet that 2026's most speculative corners are still being repriced. For the week ahead: Xi Jinping's Sept 24-25 Washington visit is the dominant catalyst, with rare-earth export licensing squarely on the agenda 47 days ahead of the Nov 10 export-control deadline, alongside flash PMI data Sept 23, Micron's Sept 25 earnings, and Starship Flight 14's latest target of NET Sept 28. — Updated Sep 21, 2026; refreshed weekly.
