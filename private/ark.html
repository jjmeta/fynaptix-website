01 // THE THESIS
What ARK Actually Believes
CORE IDEA
Disruptive innovation is under-owned
ARK's founding claim: traditional benchmarks are built around the past, so genuinely new platforms sit outside index weights until they're already huge. They run concentrated, high-conviction, actively-managed funds to own them early.
FIVE PLATFORMS
Everything maps to five technologies
AI, Energy Storage, Robotics, Multiomic Sequencing, and Public Blockchains — and crucially, ARK argues these converge, each accelerating the others rather than growing in isolation.
WRIGHT'S LAW
Costs fall on a schedule
ARK's analytical engine: for every cumulative doubling of units produced, cost falls a predictable percentage. They use it to project launch costs (-17% per doubling), battery, sequencing and inference costs — then size the market that cheapness unlocks.
TIME HORIZON
Five years, not five quarters
Every number on this page is a 2030 projection. ARK explicitly accepts high short-term volatility in exchange for the long arc — which is exactly why their funds swing far harder than the index in both directions.
THE 2026 CLAIM
"The Great Acceleration"
This year's framing: AI is the central dynamo igniting an inflection in macroeconomic growth, not just tech-sector growth. ARK forecasts innovation's share of global equity market cap rising toward ~35% growth-driven dominance by 2030.
WHY IT MATTERS HERE
It overlaps your hub almost exactly
ARK's platforms map onto seven of your nine sectors. Reading their thesis is a useful stress-test: where do you agree, where are they too optimistic, and what are they seeing that your research isn't?

From $1.43T today, growing 19–56% annually depending on adoption speed. Software-development inference costs fell 91% in eight months. ARK argues hours worked fall without long-term unemployment rising.

~$16T

~63% CAGR from ~$2T, built bottom-up from TAM penetration: institutional allocation, digital gold (~40% of gold's $24.4T cap), EM safe haven, nation-state and corporate treasuries. US ETFs + public companies already hold ~12% of supply.

06 // HOW TO READ ARK
Strengths, Blind Spots & the Honest Scorecard
Where ARK is genuinely useful
Their cost-curve work (Wright's Law) is rigorous and often directionally right — sequencing, battery and inference costs really did collapse. The Big Ideas report is one of the best free primary-source syntheses of frontier tech available, with ~6 pages of citations.
The track record is genuinely mixed
ARKK's 2020 run and subsequent drawdown means many investors' actual dollar-weighted returns were poor even across periods when the fund's own returns looked acceptable. Being early and being wrong are hard to tell apart in real time.
Forecasts assume everything compounds
The models chain optimistic assumptions: adoption × penetration × take rate × multiple. Each is defensible alone; multiplied together they produce headline numbers ($34T, $1.2 quadrillion) that need heavy discounting.
Concentration and reflexivity
ARK funds are concentrated in illiquid mid-caps and sometimes own large percentages of a company's float — which can amplify moves in both directions during inflows and redemptions.
The right way to use this page
As a thesis-generator and counterpoint, not a shopping list. Where ARK's number and your sector dashboard disagree, that gap is the interesting research question — log it in your Decision Journal.
Note the quantum caveat
Worth flagging for your Quantum dashboard: ARK explicitly says quantum computing is "unlikely to be disruptive for 20 to 40 years" — a striking dissent from the sector's current valuations.
