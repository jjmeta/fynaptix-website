02 // HOW TO CHOOSE
Selection Principles
CONCENTRATION
Check the top-10 weight
BOTZ-style funds (~40%+ in five names) behave like a handful of stocks; ROBO-style equal-weighting actually diversifies. Concentration isn't bad — it's a choice to make deliberately.
OVERLAP
Watch for duplicate exposure
NVDA appears in robotics, AI, and quantum funds simultaneously. Holding three thematic ETFs can mean tripling the same mega-caps rather than diversifying.
COSTS
Thematic ERs run 0.4–1.0%
Versus ~0.03–0.10% for broad index cores. Fine for satellite positions; expensive for core money.
LIQUIDITY
Small AUM funds can close
Thematic ETFs under ~$50M AUM get liquidated regularly — a taxable event forced on you. Prefer funds with established AUM.
