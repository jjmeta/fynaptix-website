01 // THE THESIS
What Shield Capital Actually Believes
FOUNDING BELIEF
America's tech edge is not guaranteed
Shield Capital was founded on the belief that the US technological advantage in national security requires active defence by private capital. The Pentagon's procurement system cannot move fast enough to absorb commercial technology.
OPERATOR DNA
The founders have seen what failure looks like
Philip Bilden's background in private equity and national security finance, and Raj Shah's background as a fighter pilot and leader of the Pentagon's Defence Innovation Unit (DIU), give Shield a perspective that pure Silicon Valley funds don't have.
DUAL-USE FIRST
Commercial viability is a national security requirement
Shield specifically avoids single-customer (government-only) businesses. Technologies that succeed commercially can be sustained by market competition; government-only tech stagnates.
AUTONOMY THESIS
Uncrewed systems will define the next conflict
The lesson of Ukraine — that cheap drones can defeat expensive armour at scale — has accelerated conviction in autonomy. Shield backs companies building software for uncrewed systems, autonomous decision-making and drone swarm coordination.
CYBER & INTELLIGENCE
The information environment is a battlefield
Shield backs companies in offensive and defensive cyber, open-source intelligence (OSINT), and signals intelligence. The government customer is often the only entity with the data needed to train the AI.
PATIENT CAPITAL
Government procurement takes years
Shield explicitly structures its fund with longer hold periods because defence contracts take 3-5 years from first customer conversation to material revenue. Most VCs can't survive that timeline; Shield is built for it.

05 // HOW TO READ SHIELD CAPITAL
Strengths, Blind Spots & the Honest Assessment
Differentiated access is real
Raj Shah ran the Pentagon's Defence Innovation Unit. That network access is not replicable by a San Francisco fund writing its first defence check. Shield gets deal flow that others don't see.
The timing is right
Ukraine, Taiwan, AI proliferation — the urgency around national security technology has never been higher. The US government is actively trying to award more contracts to non-traditional defence companies.
The fund is young
Founded in 2021, Shield has a short track record. Defence procurement cycles mean most investments haven't yet converted to revenue at scale.
Government concentration risk
Most Shield portfolio companies rely heavily on government contracts. A change in administration priorities or a DoD budget cut can materially affect all of them simultaneously.
How to use Shield for your hub
Shield Capital is the best lens on the national security side of your Defense Tech dashboard. Track their portfolio — Palantir, Shield AI, Vannevar — as leading indicators of what the US government is buying in defence AI and autonomy.
