04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Capital intensity
Rockets and constellations burn billions before returns — Stoke Space's Series D alone has grown to $1.34B raised before its first launch; dilution is chronic among small caps (RKLB's Iridium roll-up, ASTS's $1B convertible notes).
Government concentration
Much of the sector's revenue is NASA/DoD — SDA's Golden Dome build-out alone is driving billions in LHX/Sierra Space awards; budget shifts or program cancellations reprice backlogs fast.
SpaceX absorbs the value
A dominant incumbent with launch-cost leverage can compress everyone else's margins — even SpaceX itself needed a 92% revenue surge and improving Starlink economics just to narrow its Q2 net loss to $541M, and shares are still down ~41% from their post-IPO high despite that beat; xAI's AI cash burn continues to weigh on consolidated results.
Binary mission risk
One failed landing or launch can halve a small-cap (see lunar-lander names); Blue Origin's own May 28 pad explosion and this week's root-cause finding (a single oxygen valve) show how fast hardware failure can set a program back months. SpaceX's own prospectus flags "key man" dependence on Elon Musk.
