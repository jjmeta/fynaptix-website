04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Capital intensity
Rockets and constellations burn billions before returns; dilution is chronic among small caps.
Government concentration
Much of the sector's revenue is NASA/DoD; budget shifts reprice backlogs fast.
SpaceX absorbs the value
A dominant incumbent with launch-cost leverage can compress everyone else's margins — and SpaceX itself trades near 70-99x sales, ~15x the Nasdaq-100's multiple, with 2025 free cash flow of -$13.8B and Q1 2026 FCF of -$9.1B.
Binary mission risk
One failed landing or launch can halve a small-cap (see lunar-lander names); SpaceX's own prospectus flags "key man" dependence on Elon Musk.
