04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Capital intensity
Rockets and constellations burn billions before returns — Stoke Space's Series E closed at ~$1B on Aug 21 on top of $1.34B already spent pre-launch; dilution and debt are chronic among small caps (RKLB's $3.6B Iridium bridge facility, ASTS's $1B convertible notes), and ASTS is only now steadying after a rate-driven single-day drop — a reminder of how exposed debt-funded builders are to the rate cycle even when the underlying operating story is intact.
Government concentration
Much of the sector's revenue is NASA/DoD — the Golden Dome build-out keeps expanding (the MDA's SHIELD vehicle now spans 2,100+ awardees, L3Harris/Sierra Space's $1.75B Tranche 3 award), but a securities-claims investigation still sits alongside the Aug 17 CEO ouster at prime contractor L3Harris, a reminder that governance and sentiment risk sit alongside budget risk in this concentrated customer base.
SpaceX absorbs the value / sector repricing
A dominant incumbent with launch-cost leverage can compress everyone else's margins — SPCX closed at $147.95 on Sept 5 but remains ~34% below its post-IPO high, and its next real catalyst (Starship Flight 14) is now firmly dated Sept 15. Meanwhile record fundamentals across RKLB, RDW, LUNR and Firefly this earnings season haven't stopped RKLB shares from sitting ~58% off their highs and below the $67.50 floor of its own Iridium collar — a widening gap between operating results and price that cuts both ways.
Binary mission risk
One failed test or slipped date can move a small-cap sharply: Rocket Lab's Neutron — once tracking a 2026 debut — now looks more likely to slip to 2027 after a January tank-pressure-test rupture, even with 400+ Archimedes hot fires logged; Starship Flight 14 is a firm near-term test of SpaceX's own execution on Sept 15; and Blue Origin's still-unresolved New Glenn root-cause investigation shows how a single hardware failure can stall a program ahead of its promised year-end return to flight. SpaceX's own prospectus flags "key man" dependence on Elon Musk.
