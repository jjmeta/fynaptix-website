03 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
China dominates cells — and is now out-competing itself abroad too
CATL holds the global No.1 position in BESS shipments for January–June and storage now makes up nearly 20% of its H1 2026 revenue, but the competitive pressure is no longer just China-vs-the-West — BYD and Sungrow just won an 11.275GWh Masdar (Abu Dhabi) storage deal that had reportedly been expected to go to CATL, showing domestic rivalry is also compressing terms. CATL's Naxtra sodium-ion cells are still on track for cost parity with LFP by year-end, with first customer deliveries beginning this month — a cost-curve threat layered on top of existing scale dominance.
Tariffs, FEOC rules, and now FERC co-location add a third axis of pressure
Section 301 tariffs on Chinese-origin battery energy storage systems sit at 25% (roughly 55% combined with existing duties), and FEOC content thresholds now disqualify projects leaning too heavily on restricted foreign entities, starting at 55% in 2026 and tightening to 75% after 2029. Layered on top, a live FERC proceeding directing PJM to rewrite co-located load rules is still working through implementation in 2026 — transmission cost allocation under the new framework directly affects grid-services revenue, a primary component of return on invested capital for data-center-adjacent storage projects like NRGV's and Cummins'.
Margin compression is spreading beyond Fluence
Fluence has now taken three sell-side downgrades in ten days — Barclays (Sept 3), Mizuho (Sept 9, on $20M of actual cost overruns), and Piper Sandler (Sept 8, flagging FY27 EBITDA estimates ~60% below consensus) — with shares down more than half YTD despite a record $6.4B backlog. Stem's slide to a fresh 52-week low on Sept 11, despite a fifth consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA, suggests the market is now discounting execution and top-line risk across the software/optimization layer too, not just hardware integrators.
Solid-state timelines — and the Chinese threat is catching up
QuantumScape has had no fresh catalysts since its July Q2 print and June Honda R&D agreement, and Truist's $6 price target still stands. Meanwhile CATL and BYD are both publicly targeting small-scale solid-state trial production by 2027 — with no production license, no volume commitment, and no revenue at QuantumScape, the Western first-mover advantage in solid-state is no longer assumed, just asserted.
