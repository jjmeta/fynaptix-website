03 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
China dominates cells
CATL and BYD control battery-cell economics; Western integrators compete on software and services, not hardware cost.
Policy whiplash
Tariffs and IRA changes can reshape project economics overnight; the sector is policy-levered in both directions.
Margin compression
Falling battery prices grow volumes but squeeze integrator margins; software attach is the differentiator.
Solid-state timelines
QS-type names have promised commercialization for years; lithium-ion's cost curve keeps moving the goalposts.
