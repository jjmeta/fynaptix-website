03 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
China dominates cells
CATL (35%) and BYD (14%) control over half of global battery shipments; a 4-hour BESS can be quoted near $65/kWh in China vs $230–320/kWh in the US. Western integrators compete on software and services, not hardware cost.
Policy whiplash
Section 301 tariffs now extend to grid-scale BESS; a House-passed bill would bar DHS from buying CATL-linked batteries starting Oct 2027. ~21 GWh of planned US cell capacity was already cancelled after the 2025 tariff shock.
Margin compression
Falling battery prices grow volumes but squeeze integrator margins; software attach is the differentiator.
Solid-state timelines
Honda's new QuantumScape JV is a vote of confidence, but commercial-scale solid-state production remains years out; lithium-ion's cost curve keeps moving the goalposts.
