03 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
China dominates cells
CATL (40.2%) and BYD (14.4%) hold over half of global EV/storage battery shipments through May 2026 (CATL installs +23% YoY); together they command ~64% of China's domestic market. Western integrators compete on software and services, not hardware cost.
Policy whiplash
Section 301 duties on non-EV lithium-ion (grid BESS) cells jumped from 7.5% to 25% effective Jan 1, 2026, pushing the effective combined US import tariff on Chinese ESS batteries to ~58.4% (from 40.9%); new FEOC rules cap Chinese-sourced content at 45% of manufacturing cost for ITC eligibility.
Margin compression
Tesla's own Q2 print is fresh evidence: energy-segment margin nearly halved QoQ (39.5% → 20.4%) on a warranty charge and a lapsed tariff benefit, plus falling industrial-storage ASPs. Software attach remains the differentiator for integrators.
Solid-state timelines
QuantumScape's Honda pact and Straubel board addition are votes of confidence, but the company is still burning ~$65M/quarter with $859M liquidity that analysts say must last until "late this decade" before meaningful revenue arrives.
