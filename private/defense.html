04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Public-market valuations still elevated
The US Aerospace & Defense industry continues to trade well above historical multiples. Palantir has climbed to ~$186.29 (Aug 28) on raised price targets, yet Cathie Wood trimmed $27.2M of PLTR in August even as the stock rises — a reminder that conviction is far from unanimous at these prices.
Rate-driven rotation is hitting high-multiple small caps hard
30-year Treasury yields near 19-year highs have twice this month triggered a coordinated "drone trade unwind" across the small-cap complex — Aug 20 (Ondas -7%, Kratos -5%) and again Aug 28 (Unusual Machines -9%, Ondas -6%, Red Cat -5%) — with no company-specific news behind either move. Kratos is now down ~20% YTD despite two guidance raises this summer.
Winning a contract doesn't guarantee funding follows through
Golden Dome's own program chief warned in mid-August the shield could halt as early as October without new congressional appropriation — Congress funded FY2026 through reconciliation rather than the base budget, and a continuing resolution would leave FY2027 with no baseline — despite L3Harris and Sierra Space just banking $1.75B combined in fresh missile-tracking satellite awards.
Governance and program-execution risk hits even established primes
Two weeks after L3Harris ousted CEO Chris Kubasik with no further explanation offered, the uncertainty remains unresolved, and Rheinmetall's F126 frigate cancellation is still working through its FY26 guidance months later — proof that record backlogs don't insulate a prime from sudden leadership or program shocks.
