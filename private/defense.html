04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Public-market valuations already stretched
The US Aerospace & Defense industry trades around 32-33x trailing earnings (CSIMarket) — well above its historical range — while closed private defense-tech deals ran at a median ~18.9x EBITDA / 3.7x revenue through Q1 2026, the richest revenue multiple in four years. Even this week's strong prints (PLTR, KTOS, AVAV) came with sharp round-trips or price-target cuts, a sign public investors are pricing risk into the growth names even as headline multiples climb.
Capital outrunning production capacity
Defense tech VC funding topped $14.6B in barely five months of 2026, already a full-year record. Commentators have warned capital is moving faster than governments can convert new technology into recurring production orders — a mismatch that could leave late-stage private rounds overextended.
Budget politics
FY27 requests aren't appropriations; NATO's 5%-of-GDP-by-2035 pledge is real money on paper (Germany to €162B/3.2% GDP by 2029, France to 2.25% GDP in 2026) but still depends on political follow-through year after year.
Downselects hit even the story stocks
Kratos' exclusion from the June CCA Increment 1 production award (Anduril and General Atomics won instead) is exactly the single-program dependency risk this page has flagged — a beat-and-raise Q2 wasn't enough to stop Cantor cutting its price target. Rheinmetall's Leonardo Iveco-truck deal, tidy-looking months ago, is now snagged by a Leonardo CEO change and rival bidders. AeroVironment separately still faces multiple securities-fraud class actions despite its Locust contract win.
