04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Public-market valuations already stretched
The US Aerospace & Defense industry trades at ~44.8x P/E vs its 3-year average of ~32.1x, while early-stage defense-tech startups fetch 17-50x revenue in private rounds — even as pure-plays AVAV and KTOS sit 40%+ off their highs. The gap between private enthusiasm and public repricing keeps widening.
Capital outrunning production capacity
VCs deployed a reported $19.8B into defense tech in Q1 2026 alone across 262 deals. Commentators (Fortune) warn capital is moving faster than governments can convert new technology into recurring production orders — a mismatch that could leave late-stage private rounds overextended.
Budget politics
FY27 requests aren't appropriations; NATO's new 5%-of-GDP-by-2035 pledge and US pressure on allies over spending commitments add near-term political and execution risk to the rearmament timeline.
Program-cancellation risk hits primes too
Germany's abrupt cancellation of Rheinmetall's €10B+ F126 frigate program (after €2.3B already sunk) showed single-program dependency isn't just a pure-play problem — though Rheinmetall's subsequent record Q2 print suggests primes can absorb the hit. AeroVironment separately faces multiple securities-fraud class actions.
