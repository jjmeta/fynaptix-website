04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Public-market valuations still elevated
Michael Burry renewed his Palantir warning Sep 2, arguing the company behaves more like a consulting business than software and that its ~$432B market cap could eventually fall below $100B, citing a ~32% deferred-revenue ratio nearly matching Accenture's and $265M of Q2 stock-based comp — shares still rebounded 7.8% the next day on unrelated PwC partnership news, showing how sentiment-driven the stock remains.
AeroVironment's restatement is a live example of defense-tech execution risk
A January stop-work order on the SCAR/BADGER satellite-antenna program triggered a $151.3M goodwill impairment; an error in that calculation then forced AeroVironment to restate financials, understating its nine-month net loss by $87.3M, and securities-fraud class actions are now proceeding into Sep 9 earnings — a reminder that fast-growing defense-tech names can carry real accounting and disclosure risk alongside the growth story.
The defense-tech VC boom still looks like a bubble
Anduril's targeted $100B round remains unclosed seven weeks after first being reported, and CEO Brian Schimpf has now confirmed the company is delaying its own IPO because it's "bad" to list "in the middle of a hype cycle" — even as hypersonic-missile startup Castelion pulled in the week's largest fresh VC round, showing capital is still chasing the space at a pace some insiders themselves call unsustainable.
The Sep 1 stopgap and Golden Dome's funding fragility just push the fight to December
The continuing resolution funds government (and defense) through Dec 11 at FY26 levels but settles nothing on FY27 appropriations, the $54.6B DAWG autonomy line item, or the CCA Increment 2 downselect; Golden Dome's own lead, Gen. Guetlein, warned Aug 12 that the missile-shield program is at risk because it depends on reconciliation funding with no FY26 topline behind it if that process stalls.
Governance and program-execution risk hits even established primes
Nearly three weeks after L3Harris ousted CEO Chris Kubasik with no further explanation offered, the uncertainty remains unresolved, and Rheinmetall's F126 frigate cancellation is still weighing on shares (down to ~€1,034 despite a record Q2 book-to-bill and fresh Kassel-hub and Hensoldt news) — proof that record backlogs don't insulate a prime from sudden leadership or program shocks.
