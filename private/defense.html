04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Private/public valuation gap widening
Anduril is in talks near a $100B valuation — over 3x its mark from a year ago — while public pure-plays AVAV and KTOS are both down 40%+ YTD despite record backlogs. Public markets are already repricing even as private rounds accelerate.
Budget politics
FY27 requests aren't appropriations; NATO summit in Turkey and US pressure on allies over spending commitments add near-term political risk to the rearmament timeline.
Program-cancellation risk hits primes too
Germany's abrupt cancellation of Rheinmetall's €10B+ F126 frigate program (after €2.3B already sunk) shows single-program dependency isn't just a pure-play problem — established primes can be blindsided too.
Program concentration & litigation
Pure-plays often depend on a handful of contracts; a single loss (e.g., CCA downselect) can reprice a stock sharply. AeroVironment now also faces multiple securities-fraud class actions.
