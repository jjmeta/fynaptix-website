04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Public-market valuations still elevated
The US Aerospace & Defense industry continues to trade well above historical multiples. Palantir has rebounded to ~$179.80 even after its 52-week-high pullback, and Cathie Wood trimmed $27.2M of PLTR in August even as sell-side targets rise — a reminder that conviction is far from unanimous at these prices.
Capital outrunning production — and now diluting shareholders
Defense tech VC funding topped $14.6B in barely five months of 2026. On the public side, Kratos itself just raised $1.4B in a follow-on offering to fund growth — a reminder that even profitable, guidance-raising names are tapping capital markets hard, diluting existing holders in the process.
Governance and program-execution risk hits even established primes
L3Harris ousted CEO Chris Kubasik on Aug 17 over an unspecified conduct violation, and Rheinmetall's F126 frigate cancellation (Germany chose TKMS instead) is still working through its FY26 guidance months later — proof that record backlogs don't insulate a prime from sudden leadership or program shocks.
Budget and trade politics
FY27 requests aren't appropriations, and NATO's 5%-of-GDP pledge still depends on year-by-year political follow-through. The Aug 13 tariff proclamation takes effect around Sep 3 and looks largely priced into the smaller drone names already; a 9-vendor CCA Increment 2 downselect remains open with no clear selection criteria yet disclosed.
