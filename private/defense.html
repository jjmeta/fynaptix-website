04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Valuation re-rating debate
Early-stage defense startups fetch 17x-50x revenue; Anduril, Shield AI and Saronic have each roughly doubled valuation within a year. AVAV and KTOS are both down 40%+ YTD despite record backlogs — public markets are already repricing.
Budget politics
FY27 requests aren't appropriations; NATO summit in Turkey and US pressure on allies over spending commitments add near-term political risk to the rearmament timeline.
Legacy vs new-tech split
If money flows to legacy platforms over AI/drones/EW, software-first names underperform expectations.
Program concentration
Pure-plays often depend on a handful of contracts; a single loss (e.g., CCA downselect) can reprice a stock sharply.
