04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Public-market valuations already stretched
The US Aerospace & Defense industry trades around 32-33x trailing earnings (CSIMarket) — well above its historical range. Palantir has already eased from a $207.52 52-week high to ~$174 despite raised guidance, and even Anduril's own CEO has warned publicly against IPO'ing "in the middle of a hype cycle" — a notable caution signal from an insider riding the sector's biggest private markup.
Capital outrunning production capacity
Defense tech VC funding topped $14.6B in barely five months of 2026, already a full-year record. Commentators have warned capital is moving faster than governments can convert new technology into recurring production orders — a mismatch that could leave late-stage private rounds overextended.
Budget and trade politics
FY27 requests aren't appropriations; NATO's 5%-of-GDP-by-2035 pledge is real money on paper but still depends on political follow-through year after year. The new Aug 13 tariff proclamation (up to 100% on imported drones) also raises input-cost and retaliation risk for primes with global supply chains, even as it favors pure-play US drone makers.
Strong headline prints can still hide execution risk
Rheinmetall's Q2 profit nearly doubled, yet the company cut FY26 revenue guidance days later on a naval-programme setback — proof that a strong topline doesn't guarantee a clean guide. Kratos remains excluded from the June CCA Increment 1 production award (Anduril and General Atomics won instead) despite this week's new contract wins. Red Cat's 527% revenue growth still came with a consensus miss and a widened net loss. AeroVironment separately still faces multiple unresolved securities-fraud class actions.
