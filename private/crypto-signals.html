05 // HOW TO USE THESE SIGNALS
Reading the Cards
Entry Zone
Don't market-order in. Wait for price to reach the zone, then look for a 15m confirmation candle close before entering. Zones absorb volatility without you chasing.
R:R Ratio
Risk-to-reward. A 3:1 means the target is 3× further from entry than the stop. Only take setups with R:R above 2.0. This is what makes a positive expectancy system even at 40% win rate.
Position Sizing
Kelly formula: f = (bp − q) / b, where b = R:R, p = estimated win rate, q = 1−p. Use half-Kelly until a 20+ trade track record is established. Never risk more than 2% of capital on any single trade.
Status Badges
WATCHING = setup is valid but price hasn't reached the entry zone yet. ACTIVE = price is in the zone, position can be opened. TRIGGERED = entered, position is live.
Macro Gates
Some setups are gated on external events (NVDA AUG 26, Jackson Hole AUG 27–29). Don't enter a gated signal before its trigger event confirms — you'd be adding risk ahead of a known binary.
