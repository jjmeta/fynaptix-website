04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Commodity cycles cut both ways
Lithium's 2023–25 crash shows how fast oversupply can crush miners; China lithium carbonate just hit a fresh 5-month low on 2027 restart/glut fears.
China can flood the market
Beijing has historically dumped supply to crush Western rare-earth economics; price floors help but don't cover everyone.
Juniors dilute
Developers without cash flow fund construction via repeated equity raises; USAR/LAC-type names carry meaningful dilution risk.
Policy dependency
The thesis leans on sustained government support; a policy shift or trade détente removes a key prop.
