03 // GEOPOLITICS
The China Factor
EXPORT CONTROLS
Beijing weaponizing processing dominance
Ex-China rare-earth prices have exploded since Beijing's April-2025 controls: European dysprosium up ~8x to ~$2,250/kg, terbium ~5x, yttrium oxide up to ~140x to ~$1,100/kg; the NdPr alloy benchmark alone rose 21.4% in the month to Jul 1. Shipments of yttrium, dysprosium and terbium into the US remain down ~50% versus pre-control levels.
ESCALATION
Truce getting tested
Beijing's Jun 22 entity-list additions (MP Materials, USA Rare Earth, among 10 US firms) are testing the fragile Oct-2025 Busan truce, which runs through Nov 10, 2026; analysts are increasingly describing a "rare earth clash 2.0" as tit-for-tat moves resume ahead of the deadline.
ONSHORING
Washington's playbook
Project Vault's $10B EXIM loan + ~$2B private capital remains the umbrella policy, layered with company-level support: DoD's price floor and $140M EBITDA guarantee for MP's 10X plant, USA Rare Earth's $1.6B DoC stake, and Energy Fuels' now-conditional $725M Office of Strategic Capital loan for a terbium/dysprosium build-out.
04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Commodity cycles cut both ways
Albemarle's blowout Q2 (lithium prices +61%, EBITDA +155%) came days after it deferred hard-rock expansion capex on Aug 1 — and China's domestic lithium carbonate spot price still slipped to a ~6-month low the same week the results came out. One strong quarter doesn't confirm the cycle has turned.
China can flood the market
Beijing has both weaponized scarcity (yttrium oxide up ~140x since the April-2025 controls) and, historically, dumped supply when it suited Beijing's interests; the Nov 10 truce deadline is the key swing factor either way.
Juniors dilute
Lithium Americas just layered a $175M Yorkville convertible-debenture facility — a relatively expensive, potentially dilutive structure — on top of its DoE loan; USA Rare Earth is issuing 126.85M new shares (34% of the combined company) to close Serra Verde. Building a rare-earth refinery runs $500M–$1B+, and names without direct sovereign grants keep diluting to get there.
Policy dependency
Energy Fuels' $725M OSC loan is still conditional, not closed; the onshoring trade leans on sustained federal appetite and a truce Beijing has already tested once this quarter (Jun 22 entity-list additions). A policy reversal or a durable détente both cut against the thesis, just in different ways.
