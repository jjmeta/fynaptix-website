03 // GEOPOLITICS
The China Factor
EXPORT CONTROLS
Beijing weaponizing processing dominance
China's Rare Earth Price Index held at 272.2 on Jul 14 (terbium oxide ~$1,033/kg, NdPr oxide ~$114/kg domestic); the Oct-2025 US-China truce and tariff suspension run through Nov 10, 2026 — a key overhang for ex-China supply.
ESCALATION
Enforcement gets more active
Beijing's Jun 22 entity list (MP Materials, USA Rare Earth) is now paired with MOFCOM's formal violation-reporting mechanism; two Japanese nationals were detained in Dalian in May 2026 on alleged rare-earth-related smuggling charges, signaling tighter enforcement.
ONSHORING
Washington's playbook
Project Vault's $10B EXIM loan + ~$2B private capital is structured around OEM offtake commitments (GM and others), not central planning; layered on top is ~$2.9B in direct DoD/DoE/EXIM funding plus Energy Fuels' new $725M DoW loan and USA Rare Earth's $1.6B DoC stake.
04 // BEAR CASE
Risks & Counterarguments
Commodity cycles cut both ways
Lithium's 2023–25 crash shows how fast oversupply can crush miners; Albemarle is now deferring hard-rock expansion capex to protect its balance sheet until prices stabilize, and Chile policy shifts are hitting SQM hardest across the Lithium Triangle.
China can flood the market
Beijing has historically dumped supply to crush Western rare-earth economics; price floors help but don't cover everyone.
Juniors dilute
Developers without cash flow fund construction via repeated equity raises; USAR/LAC-type names carry meaningful dilution risk.
Policy dependency
The thesis leans on sustained government support; a policy shift or trade détente removes a key prop.
