01 // THE THESIS
What 8VC Actually Believes
FOUNDING BELIEF
The best investors have built things
Joe Lonsdale co-founded Palantir at 21 with Peter Thiel and then founded 8VC a decade later. The fund's thesis: operators who have built complex, regulated, mission-critical systems understand what it takes to sell to governments, hospitals and banks.
PALANTIR LINEAGE
The PayPal Mafia of the defence-tech world
A disproportionate number of 8VC portfolio companies are founded by Palantir alumni. Palantir taught a generation of engineers how to build systems that work in the most demanding real-world environments.
HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS
US healthcare is broken and profitable to fix
Joe Lonsdale has spoken extensively about healthcare as a broken market where incumbents have every incentive to preserve the status quo. 8VC backs companies using AI and data to reduce administrative waste and improve clinical decision-making.
GOVERNMENT TECH
Governments are the world's biggest under-served software customers
Governments run on software built in the 1990s or earlier. 8VC's govtech thesis: selling modern software to state, local and federal governments is an enormous market that most VCs ignore because procurement is slow and complex.
LONSDALE NETWORK
Texas relocation and political engagement
Joe Lonsdale's move from San Francisco to Austin has created a network of conservative and libertarian founders and LPs that differs from the mainstream Silicon Valley VC ecosystem. This network generates deal flow in defence, energy and govtech.
CAPITAL EFFICIENCY
They look for companies that cannot be easily replicated
8VC looks for defensibility: regulatory moats, proprietary government relationships, unique datasets or deep integration into existing institutional workflows. Capital-efficient businesses in slow-moving sectors are structurally underpriced.
Key Partners: Joe Lonsdale · Jake Medwell · Ali Rosenthal · Drew Oetting · David Avalos

05 // HOW TO READ 8VC
Strengths, Blind Spots & the Honest Assessment
Operator-investor DNA is genuinely valuable in hard sectors
Selling software to the Pentagon, to hospital systems, or to state governments requires domain knowledge that most VCs don't have. 8VC's partners have been inside these systems as founders.
Palantir co-founding is a singular qualification
Palantir has become one of the most important technology companies in the US national security apparatus and is now crossing over into commercial enterprise AI.
Political positioning creates concentration
Joe Lonsdale's explicitly stated political views mean that 8VC's network is concentrated in a specific political and cultural ecosystem. Some of the best founder talent and some LP segments don't overlap with that ecosystem.
Company-level conflicts of interest
Lonsdale co-founded Palantir, Addepar and OpenGov — three of 8VC's most significant investments. That alignment can be a strength (conviction) or a concern (fiduciary complexity).
How to use 8VC for your hub
Palantir is your primary indicator for defence AI adoption. 8VC's broader network — Anduril, defence AI spinouts — is the most connected private view into US national security technology priorities.
