AI Readiness: Classification First
Classification is the foundation of safe AI deployment. Before Claude or Copilot can safely access your data, every document and dataset it can reach should carry a sensitivity label. Without classification, AI tools cannot distinguish public-facing content from board-level confidential material. Your roadmap prioritises this before expanding AI access.
